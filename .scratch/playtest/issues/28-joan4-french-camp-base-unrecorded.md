**Reported:** 2026-10-23, found while grilling [#23](23-land-defence-unpaid.md)

**Status:** needs-triage

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

Joan 4's "AP Give Base" trigger hands P1 the French Camp from Gaia: a Town Center and a Mill. The
logic records neither. `Joan4StartingState` sets `has_vils` and the camp's units on
`AP_JOAN_4_FRENCH_CAMP`, but no `starts_with_building`, which defaults to `False_()` for every
building. So `has_base()` never sees the standing Town Center and asks for the Town Center items
instead, and the Mill is never a standing dropsite.

Precedent: Attila 1 (`has_bledas_camp`) and Genghis 2 (`tayichiud_base`) gate
`starts_with_building` on the item that hands the camp over.
