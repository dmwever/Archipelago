## Report

The largest groups left on `test/never_bare_allowlist.txt` at #40's close (691 lines):

- **Defeat, guard, goal, Victory and Complete locations** (~335 lines), e.g.
  `A Barbarian Betrothal: Can Defeat Burgundy: A Barbarian Betrothal can train a Capped Ram (unpaid: stone)`,
  and the dock guards' Fire Galleys `(unpaid: food, stone)`. Paid `ScenarioUnitLogic._field` asks
  `ages.can_pay_for(unit.age)`, the age price alone, not `ages.can_advance`. #42 left it.
- **The army holding open ground inside the at-scale routes' tier gates** (~200 lines), e.g.
  `A Life of Revenge runs Villagers gathering gold with a Base at a built Mining Camp: A Life of Revenge can train a Camel Rider (unpaid: stone)`.
  `ScenarioMilitaryLogic._can_field_some` asks the line's first tier structurally plus *some* of its
  training cost. It pays no advance (the stone is the advance's Town Center, the wood its age-up
  buildings).
- **Feudal walls and towers as a defence** (4 lines, Walls of Constantinople's fishing routes:
  `can build a Stone Wall (unpaid: food)`). `ScenarioBuildingLogic._can_defend_with` pays a building
  below Castle *some* of its cost and nothing for its age.

Found during triage, and not visible to the walk: **the army defence never pays its training
building.** `ScenarioCanTrain` asks the training building structurally inside its own answer, which the
walk exempts, and the walk's cost of a unit (`cost_of`) is its own cost plus its advance, never its
building. Paid `can_train` and paid `_field` pay it (`can_pay_for_training_building`); the army defence
does not. Research buildings are costed the same way.

The Castle-age strongpoints and the second Town Center as a defence already pay their advance from the
ungated routes (#40, `_can_advance_to_castle_on_ungated_routes`), but that rule asks no Town Center.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Rulings: the maintainer, 2026-10-28 (#32's grilling, Q1-Q3,
> Q8-Q12). The glossary's **Age price** and **Defence** are the contract.

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Paid fielding asks the advance; every defence above the start age, armies included, pays
its advance out of the ungated routes; an army defence pays *some* of a training building; and the walk
costs training and research buildings.

**Current behavior**

As in the Report: paid fielding pays the age price but not the advance, the army defence pays neither,
walls and towers pay no age, the Castle-age advance for defences asks no Town Center, the army defence
pays no training building, and the walk cannot see training or research buildings.

**Desired behavior**

1. **Paid fielding asks the advance.** `ScenarioUnitLogic._field` (paid) asks
   `ages.can_advance(unit.age)` in place of `ages.can_pay_for(unit.age)`. Nothing inside the economy
   asks paid fielding, so this cannot loop.
2. **The age price merges into the advance.** After (1), the age price's only caller is
   `ScenarioCanAdvance.answer`. Inline the price table there and delete `ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for`.
   Its fixed-force and `start_age` guards are dead there, since `can_advance` returns before building
   the question. Tests: `test_base_through_price.py`'s `paid_climb` helper drops its `can_pay_for`
   term (`can_research` already pays the price through `can_advance`); `test_feudal_age_food.py`
   asserts the Feudal price through `can_advance(FEUDAL)` on a scenario where the advance's
   buildings stand.
3. **One ungated advance rule per age, for every defence.** `_can_advance_to_castle_on_ungated_routes`
   becomes one rule over any age: True at or below `start_age`; otherwise, for each age advanced
   through, its price read from the ungated routes (Feudal: *some* food or Feudal Age Food; Castle:
   ungated food at scale and *some* gold; Imperial: as the price table), *some* wood for the age-up
   buildings, and the paid Town Center (`has_building(TOWN_CENTER)`: standing, or the Town Center
   items). It never reads the gated routes. Every defence asks it for its own age where the scenario
   starts below that age: walls, towers, every strongpoint, the second Town Center as a defence, and
   the army.
4. **The army defence** (`_can_field_some`) asks, per line: the first tier structurally, *some* of its
   training cost (as today), the rule in (3) for that tier's age, and *some* of one buildable training
   building's cost, or a training building that stands at the start.
5. **The walk costs buildings.** In `test_never_bare.py`, a `ScenarioCanTrain`'s cost adds a training
   building's cost and a `ScenarioCanResearch`'s adds a research building's, unless one of them always
   stands. Use the cheapest buildable one, so an Or of buildings is not overcharged.
6. **New sites the wider walk flags** are fixed in this ticket under the rulings above: *some* or the
   ungated rule inside the economy, the paid form outside it. A site that needs a ruling not made here
   goes on the allowlist, and the ticket lists it for triage.

**Key interfaces**

- `ScenarioUnitLogic._field`: the advance.
- `ScenarioCanAdvance.answer`: carries the price table. `ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for`: deleted.
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic._can_advance_to_castle_on_ungated_routes`: generalised over ages, with the
  paid Town Center; asked by `_can_defend_with` for every building above the start age.
- `ScenarioMilitaryLogic._can_field_some`: advance and training building.
- `test_never_bare.py`'s `cost_of`: training and research buildings.
