**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Pay, through route needs, banks and a new seed item, the capital that economy routes
stand on today for free: #32's items 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6. This touches both repos.

**Current behavior**

Trade Carts, Trade Cogs and Fish Traps ask Feudal, and relics ask Castle, with no food or gold
paid. An at-scale villager route uses a Mining Camp or Mill that nothing pays for when its seed
item is not pooled. The base buys a Town Center and three villagers, and no route pays for training
more villagers or for their Houses.

**Desired behavior**

Inside the economy, capital is paid only through a route's `needs`, a bank in its leaf, or a seed
item, never through `has_easy_source`, `has_some_source` or `can_pay`.

- **Feudal** (#32 items 1 and 2): each route that asks Feudal, in a scenario starting below Feudal,
  has a version needing `food: some` and a version on 500 banked food. Add the Feudal Age Food
  item (`SeedResources(Resource.FOOD, 500)`, id 1036), pooled by the #24 rule described in #32. In
  Ageipelago, add `FeudalAgeFood()` granting 500 food and `case 1036` in the progression-item
  switch.
- **Castle for relics** (#32 item 3): the relic routes add `food: at scale, gold: some` in a
  scenario starting below Castle.
- **Unseeded camps** (#32 item 5): the at-scale villager routes split by dropsite, like the routes
  of *some*. A built camp needs `wood: some`. Leave the second-Town-Center camp choice as it is;
  #40 splits it.
- **Villagers at scale** (#32 item 6): every route that asks for a base adds `food: some`, and
  `wood: some` where the civilisation builds Houses.
- Delete the #37 allowlist entries these changes pay for.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during triage of [#42](42-age-climb-buildings-paid.md), 2026-10-27.*

**The age's climb is capital too.** #42 ruled that reaching an age also pays for the climb into it:
a Town Center and the age-up requirement of the age before, each standing or paid for. Outside the
economy #42 builds it. Inside the economy, every route here that waits on an age the scenario
starts below also funds the climb, from outside the economy, beside the age price it already adds:

- A standing building is free.
- The Town Center is paid for by the Town Center items. They are seed items, so the route does not
  ask itself.
- An age-up building is paid for by its seed item where one is pooled (Lumber Camp, Dock, Market),
  or else by a `wood: some` need, as a Mill is funded. A building the route already stands on
  counts: a cog route's seeded Dock is one of Feudal's two.
- A Castle (an Imperial climb) is paid for by easy stone, `stone: at scale`. No route waits on
  Imperial today.
- An age the scenario starts in or past needs no climb.

#42 also widens the never-bare walk so an age costs its climb's resources. Expect this ticket's
allowlist lines to change text when #42 lands; #42 lists which.
