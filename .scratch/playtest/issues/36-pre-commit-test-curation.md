## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

#32 adds a pre-commit test that walks every location and entrance rule of two generated worlds and
fails on a structural form with no payment beside it. The maintainer: "this is the kind of test we
actually WANT to run." That raises two questions #32 kept out of its scope:

1. Which tests should pre-commit run? The preference is for invariants over a whole generated
   world, not toy scenarios with switches.
2. What is `TestEveryEasySourceAnswersAsBefore` (`test/easy_source_answers.json`) for? #32 does not
   recapture it, so it goes red when #32 lands, and this ticket decides what happens to it.

**The easy-source snapshot.** It builds 7 option sets with all three campaigns, samples 302 item
states, and asks every scenario and resource "has an easy source?" (18 × 4 = 72 questions per
state). Any flipped bit fails. It suits a refactor that claims no change in behaviour. For a
deliberate logic change it moves every time: the file has been re-committed 10 times, about once per
logic ticket. The test cannot say whether a move is right, and a diff of 302 random bit strings is
hard to review. The route catalog (`test/route_catalog.md`) shows how the routes changed in readable
form, and `TestEveryScenarioCanLive` catches a scenario that loses a resource entirely.
