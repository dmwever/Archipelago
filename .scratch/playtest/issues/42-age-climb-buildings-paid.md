## Report

#38 sets the age prices from #32: Feudal is *some* food, and Castle is easy food plus *some* gold.
With that change in the tree, two tests in `test/test_base_through_price.py` go red:

- `TestAttila1PaysForItsTownCenter::test_no_feudal_climb_without_the_town_center_items`: Attila 1
  climbs to Feudal with no Bleda's Camp and no Town Center items.
- `TestJoan3ClimbsOnlyAcrossTheWater::test_no_feudal_climb_without_boats`: under
  `start_in_dark_age`, Joan 3 climbs to Feudal without its transport.

The climb (`ScenarioAgeLogic._climb_rule`) asks for the Town Center and the two age-up buildings
only "standing or buildable". Until #38, the Feudal price was easy food, easy food needs a base,
and the base paid for the Town Center. *Some* food comes from foraging with no base, so nothing pays
for the Town Center any more.

A probe in Attila 1, with Bleda's Camp and both Town Center items withheld:

| Asked | Holds |
|---|---|
| `has_building_structurally(TOWN_CENTER)` | True |
| `ages.climb(FEUDAL)` | True |
| `ages.can_pay_for(FEUDAL)` (*some* food) | True |
| `economy.has_easy_source(FOOD)` | False |

the Reach locations. The same structural climb is also asked through `can_reach_structurally`,
inside `can_reach` and the scenario questions.

  answers, with each caller paying, as #32 has it for `has_reached`?

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Reaching an age pays for the climb into it: a Town Center and the age-up requirement
of the age before, each standing or paid for. Every rule outside the economy that asks an age asks
this one paid climb, and the never-bare walk counts the climb as part of an age's cost.

**Current behavior**

The climb (`ScenarioAgeLogic`'s climb rule) asks the age item, a Town Center "standing or
buildable", the age-up requirement "standing or buildable", and the age before through the
structural `has_reached`. Nothing pays for the Town Center. Since #38, Feudal costs *some* food,
which reaches no base, so nothing pays for it at all.

- Reach locations and `can_reach` ask the structural climb, and add the paid age-up buildings
  (`can_pay_for_age_up_buildings`) beside it as a second copy. The never-bare walk sees the
  structural copy as bare: 185 allowlist lines starting `Reach ` (160 age-up buildings, 25 Town
  Centers).
- Tech, Unit and Build locations pair the structural question with the age price only. Triage
  probed Attila 1 under `partner_trade: allies_and_enemies`, without Bleda's Camp and the Town Center
  items: `has_base()` is False, yet an Archer and a Spearman are trainable and Fletching and Scale
  Mail Armor are researchable. The never-bare walk misses this, because it does not look inside a
  scenario question's answer.
- `test_base_through_price.py`:
  `TestAttila1PaysForItsTownCenter::test_no_feudal_climb_without_the_town_center_items` and
  `TestJoan3ClimbsOnlyAcrossTheWater::test_no_feudal_climb_without_boats` are red.

**Desired behavior**

*The paid climb.* Into an age the scenario starts below, reaching it asks:

- the age item (where ages are shuffled);
- the age price (`ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for`, unchanged from #38);
- a Town Center, standing, or built and paid for with both Town Center items:
  `has_building(TOWN_CENTER)`. That still asks the Town Center building item and builders
  (villagers and the Builder profession). It asks nothing more of the base: no Villager Line, no
  Starting Villager Food, no ground (`meets_additional_base_requirements`), no House;
- the age-up requirement of the age before, each building standing or paid for (`has_building`);
  into Imperial, a Castle counts for both, paid for as a Castle (easy stone);
- the paid climb into the age before, in place of the structural `has_reached`, so each age below
  is paid once.

An age started in or past needs no climb and no price. A fixed force reaches no age.

*Who asks it.* Every rule outside the economy that asks an age asks the paid climb: the Reach
locations (the age advancement events), `can_reach`, and the age a Build, Tech or Unit location
stands on (where #38 added the age price). There is one requirement, not a structural copy plus a
paid copy. The structural climb remains only as the answer to the scenario questions
(`ScenarioHasReached`, and the age inside `ScenarioCanBuild`, `ScenarioCanResearch` and
`ScenarioCanTrain`), which never pay by design; each caller pays.

*The walk.* `test_never_bare.py` counts the climb in what an age costs:

- `ScenarioHasReached` costs the age price's resources plus the climb's: the Town Center (wood,
  stone) and the age-up buildings (wood; stone for a Castle).
- `ScenarioCanBuild`, `ScenarioCanResearch` and `ScenarioCanTrain` cost their subject's own cost
  plus the climb of their subject's age.
- An age the scenario starts in or past adds nothing.
- Matching stays by resource (#37's ruling); the Town Center items pay wood and stone, as seed items
  already do.

The walk's self-tests may be rewritten or replaced to suit. At HEAD, the wider walk should report
Attila 1's Archer location (the probe above) with stone unpaid.

*The allowlist.* Fix only this ticket's scope: Reach, `can_reach`, and Build, Tech and Unit
locations. The wider walk will change the text of out-of-scope lines (for example `(unpaid: food)`
becoming `(unpaid: food, stone, wood)`) and may add new ones: in the economy (#39, #40), and in the
untriaged groups of #32's last Comment (defeat, guard and goal rules; Complete and Victory). Rewrite
those lines to their new text, add the new ones, and list in this ticket, by group, which lines
changed or were added.

**Key interfaces**

- `ScenarioAgeLogic`: one paid climb, used by `can_reach` and the Reach locations. The climb takes
  its Town Center and age-up buildings paid. `can_pay_for_age_up_buildings`, as a separate copy
  beside a structural climb, goes away.
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_pay_for`, `ScenarioTechLogic.can_pay_for`, `ScenarioUnitLogic.can_train`:
  where each asks `ages.can_pay_for(age)` today, it asks the paid climb.
- `ScenarioHasReached` and the other structural questions: unchanged as answers.
- `test_never_bare.py`'s `BareAsks.cost`: the climb's resources added as above.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Grilling session, 2026-10-27.

Rulings worth keeping:

- **The climb's Town Center is a paid Town Center, not the base.** Joan 3's Town Center goes up on
  home ground; the base's ground across the water is not the climb's to ask (maintainer). Joan 3's
  test still goes green: without boats it has no easy wood, so its age-up buildings go unpaid
  (probed). This reverses ADR-0002's rejected "paid Town Center in the climb" option; `has_base()`
  in the climb stays rejected.
- **No villagers beyond builders.** The climb drops the Villager Line, the villager food and the
  House. Building the Town Center still asks for builders.
- **Every rule that asks an age pays for the climb**, not just Reach and `can_reach`. The probe found
  Feudal units and techs in Attila 1 with no Town Center.
- **Inside the economy the climb is capital**, funded from outside: a standing building is free, the
  Town Center is paid for by its items, an age-up building by its seed item where pooled or else
  `wood: some`, a Castle by easy stone. Built in #39 and #40, beside the age price they already add.
  The items concerned are all progression, and a seed item that is not pooled falls back to
  `wood: some`.
- **The Castle always needs easy stone.** As a defence it reads ungated easy stone. The exception is
  the stone routes at scale, which also accept a Castle paid for with *some* stone. The Fortified
  Church, Krepost and Donjon stay at *some* (maintainer: cheap enough). Built in #40, as an Or on
  the stone routes' gate, so the gate keeps no parameter.
- **One paid climb, not two copies**, and **the walk counts the climb**, rather than targeted tests
  alone: "If the tests are bad, they don't deserve to be kept" (maintainer).
- Triage claimed the walk never reached the House. Wrong: 9 allowlist lines name it, all in economy
  routes. Moved to #43.
