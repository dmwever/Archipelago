## Report

Three groups of `test/never_bare_allowlist.txt` at #40's close, about 175 lines:

- **The "... at a Town Center" routes of *some*** (Lumberjacks, Gold Miners, Stone Miners, Hunters,
  Shepherds, Foragers, Fishermen, Oyster Gatherers, plain and `, ungated`): ~128 lines of
  `<scenario> has a Town Center, standing or buildable (unpaid: stone, wood)`. The leaf asks the
  structural Town Center and nothing pays for it. A real gap.
- **Farmers and Lumberjacks with a Base**: ~16 lines of the same text. Not a real gap. The flagged
  Town Center is the Town Center branch of the dropsite Or (`has_food_dropsite`, and
  `has_wood_dropsite` inside `can_chop_some`), which is the base's own Town Center, already standing or
  paid by `has_base()`. The walk can't see that: where the Town Center stands through a scenario item
  (A Life of Revenge's Tayichi'ud Base, The Rising's French Camp), `has_base`'s Town Center is
  `Or(Town Center items, Has(<item>))`, and the walk intersects payment over an Or. Probed at the
  tree by monkeypatching; see the Comments.
- **Own Farmer**: 30 lines of `Own Farmer (...): <scenario> can build a Farm (unpaid: wood)`.
  `ScenarioUnitLogic.job_requirement` asks a job's building (Farm for Farmer, Pasture for Herder)
  structurally. In the economy the Farmers route pays its Farms with `wood: at scale`; the job
  location (`UnitLogic.can_do_job_anywhere`) pays nothing.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Rulings: the maintainer, 2026-10-28 (#32's grilling, Q4,
> Q6, Q7). The glossary's **Base** carries the dropsite ruling.

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Ask the paid Town Center on the routes of *some* at a Town Center, drop the duplicate
dropsite ask on routes that stand on the base, and pay *some* of a job's building at its location.

**Current behavior**

- The "... at a Town Center" routes of *some* ask `has_building_structurally(TOWN_CENTER)`: a Town
  Center standing or buildable, never paid.
- Routes that ask `has_base()` also ask a dropsite whose options include the Town Center (Farmers
  through `can_farm`'s food dropsite; Lumberjacks with a Base, and its second-Town-Center versions,
  through `can_chop_some`'s wood dropsite). The base's Town Center is asked twice, once paid and once
  bare.
- Own Farmer (and Own Herder, where a civilisation builds Pastures) asks its job's building
  structurally, with no payment at the location.

**Desired behavior**

1. **Routes of *some* at a Town Center** ask the paid Town Center, `ScenarioLogic.has_building(TOWN_CENTER)`:
   standing, or the two Town Center items. The Town Center's paid form never reads the economy, so it
   is safe inside a route.
2. **A route on the base asks no dropsite the Town Center already is.** Farmers drops its food
   dropsite check. Lumberjacks with a Base (every version) asks its villagers and the Lumberjack job
   without the wood dropsite. `can_chop_some` keeps its dropsite for the base-free routes. A camp the
   route asks for its own sake (`has_wood_camp`, the gold and stone camps) is not a duplicate and
   stays.
3. **A job location pays *some* of its job's building.** Where a villager job has a building, its
   location rule adds `economy.can_pay_some(building.cost)` beside `can_do_job`, as Own Builder pays
   *some* of what it builds. `job_requirement` stays structural, so the economy's Farmers route keeps
   paying through its own need and is unchanged.

**Key interfaces**

- The routes of *some* in `ScenarioRoutesLogic.villager_routes_of_some`: their Town Center ask.
- `ScenarioResourceLogic._farm` and the "Lumberjacks with a Base" routes: the dropsite asks.
- `UnitLogic.can_do_job_anywhere` (or the job location's rule): the payment.
- `ScenarioUnitLogic.job_requirement`: unchanged.
