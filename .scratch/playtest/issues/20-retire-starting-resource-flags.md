# 20: Retire the `starting_*` resource flags in favour of the scan

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split off from [#07](07-own-shepherd-without-sheep.md) during triage

**Status:** done

## Report

The `starting_*` flags on `ScenarioStartingState` say by hand whether a scenario has a resource. The
scenario resource scan now holds the same fact as counts, so there are two sources for one fact.
`JOB_TO_SCENARIOS` shows how that goes: it drifted from the scan on most jobs.

## Audit (from code)

- Fields: `starting_gold_mine`, `starting_stone_mine`, `starting_trees`, `starting_bushes`,
  `starting_hunting`, `starting_fish`, `starting_sheep`, `starting_relics` default to `True_()`;
  `starting_oysters` and `starting_whales` default to `False_()`.
- No scenario's starting state overrides any of them. Every scenario uses the defaults.
- Only the economy (`ScenarioResourceLogic`) reads them, each ANDed beside the matching scan count
  (e.g. `_herd`: `herd_count` and `starting_sheep`). The `True_` ones do nothing; the two `False_`
  ones switch oyster and whale gathering off everywhere, whatever the scan says.
- The job rule from #07 deliberately does not read them.

## Open

- Are oysters and whales off on purpose (unscanned, untested), or should the scan decide them too?
- Trees and relics: does the scan count them? If not, what replaces the flag?

### Triage Notes (2026-10-22)

**Verified from code.** The ten fields are defined on `ScenarioStartingState` and read in twelve
places, all inside `ScenarioResourceLogic`. A grep across both the apworld and the Ageipelago repo
finds no other reference: no scenario override, no test, no explain surface, no XS counterpart.

**Removing all ten is a no-op against the current scan**, including the two `False_()` ones:

- The eight `True_()` flags are identity under `&`.
- `oyster_count` and `whale_count` are `0` in every tier of every scenario. All four methods that
  read `starting_oysters` / `starting_whales` already return `False_()` from their count guard
  before the flag is reached, so the flag is a second lock on a door the scan already bolts.

**Open questions, answered:**

- **Oysters and whales** — the scan decides them today, and it says zero everywhere. The flag only
  bites the day someone scans an oyster or a whale, and on that day it would override the fresh
  scan and keep gathering off. That is the drift this issue is about, pointed at the future.
  Decision: remove them; the scan decides.
- **Trees** — the scan has *no* tree count. `ScenarioResourceCount` carries gold, stone, hunt, herd,
  bush, shore fish, deep fish, oyster, whale and relic, and `can_chop_some` is the one gatherer with
  no count guard at all. `starting_trees` was its only scenario-specific brake, and it is `True_`
  everywhere, so dropping it leaves "every scenario has wood" — the behavior today. Nothing replaces
  the flag; if a per-scenario wood limit is ever wanted, that is a `tree_count` in the scan.
- **Relics** — the scan counts them (seven across the campaigns: one scenario with 4, one with 2,
  one with 1) and `_collect_relics` already guards on `relic_count`. `starting_relics` is dead.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-22.

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Delete the ten `starting_*` resource flags so the scenario resource scan is the only source for "does this scenario have this resource"

**Current behavior:**
`ScenarioStartingState` carries ten resource flags — `starting_gold_mine`, `starting_stone_mine`,
`starting_trees`, `starting_bushes`, `starting_hunting`, `starting_fish`, `starting_sheep`,
`starting_oysters`, `starting_whales`, `starting_relics`. No scenario sets any of them; all keep
their defaults, `True_()` for eight and `False_()` for oysters and whales. Each is ANDed into the
matching gathering question in `ScenarioResourceLogic`, beside a guard on the scan's count for the
same resource. So the same fact has two sources, one of which nobody maintains — the failure mode
`JOB_TO_SCENARIOS` already demonstrated by drifting from the scan on most jobs (#07).

**Desired behavior:**
The scan is the single source. Every `starting_*` resource field is gone from
`ScenarioStartingState`, and every gathering question asks the scan's count and nothing else.

This is a behavior-preserving removal against the scan as it stands:

- The eight `True_()` flags contribute nothing to any rule.
- Oyster and whale gathering stay unreachable in every scenario, now because `oyster_count` and
  `whale_count` are `0` throughout the scan rather than because a flag forces it. If a future scan
  entry records an oyster or a whale, that gathering becomes reachable on the scan's say-so — the
  intended consequence, not an accident to guard against.
- Wood keeps being assumed available everywhere, since the scan has no tree count and
  `can_chop_some` has no count guard. Record that where the wood question lives, so the knowledge
  the flag used to imply is not lost: there is no tree count, and a per-scenario wood limit would
  mean adding one to the scan.

**Key interfaces:**

- `ScenarioStartingState`: loses the ten resource fields. Its other fields are untouched.
- `ScenarioResourceLogic`'s gathering questions — gold, stone, wood, hunt, herd, forage, shore fish,
  boat fish, oysters (both paths), whales, relics — each drops its `& starting_*` clause and keeps
  its existing scan-count guard. Rule shape is otherwise unchanged.
- `ScenarioResourceCount` and the per-tier scan data: unchanged, and now the sole authority.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] No `starting_*` resource field remains on `ScenarioStartingState`, and no rule reads one.
      *Red:* `test_scan_decides_resources.py::TestNoStartingResourceFlagRemains` (both tests).
- [x] Every gathering question that had a scan-count guard still has it; the wood question still has
      none. *Held before:* `test_scan_decides_resources.py::TestEveryGuardedQuestionKeepsItsScanGuard` (both tests).
- [x] No-op proof: with the scan unchanged, the set of reachable locations is identical before and
      after. Existing age2de tests pass with `AGEIPELAGO_PATH` set, and a fuzz run of the committed
      meta yaml shows no new fill failures. *Held before:* `test_economy_routes.py::TestEveryEasySourceAnswersAsBefore`;
      a one-off dump of every location's reachability over 302 states in 7 option configs was
      byte-identical before and after; full suite 1073 passed, 0 skipped with `AGEIPELAGO_PATH` set;
      fuzz `-r 1000 -n 1-3 -m age2de.yaml -t 90`: 0 failures, 0 timeouts.
- [x] Oyster and whale gathering are still statically unreachable in every scenario, following from
      the scan's zero counts alone. *Held before:* `test_scan_decides_resources.py::...::test_oysters_and_whales_are_unreachable_in_every_scenario`;
      *red:* `test_scan_decides_resources.py::...::test_a_scanned_oyster_or_whale_opens_its_gathering`.
- [x] The absence of a tree count — and that wood is therefore assumed available in every scenario —
      is written down where the wood question lives. *Doc:* **Resource scan** entry in
      `worlds/age2de/CONTEXT.md` (user chose CONTEXT.md over a code comment, 2026-10-23).

**Out of scope:**

- Adding a `tree_count` to the scan, or any per-scenario wood limit.
- `job_available` on `ScenarioStartingState`. It is likewise all-defaults, but #07 repurposes it to
  carry the per-job condition rather than retiring it.
- The villager job rule and `JOB_TO_SCENARIOS` (#07). That brief already states the job condition
  reads no `starting_*` flag, so the two changes do not conflict and may land in either order.
- The tier gates (`can_defend_resources`, `must_steal_base`) and the drop-off and base requirements
  on the gathering questions.
- Changing any scan count.

### Implementation (2026-10-23)

Ten fields deleted from `ScenarioStartingState`; eleven `& starting_*` clauses dropped from
`ScenarioResourceLogic`. `TestOystersGuardedByTheirOwnGold` no longer forces `starting_oysters`, and
its stale `military.boat_guards()` call (already broken at HEAD) now reads `can_guard_boats()`.
