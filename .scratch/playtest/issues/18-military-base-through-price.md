# 18: Military asks for a base directly instead of through its price

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split from [#05](05-attila1-forging-without-town-center.md) during triage

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

During #05's grilling the maintainer ruled that a base should reach locations through their price
(easy sources), not as a direct requirement. Ages, techs, units and buildings follow that once #05
lands. Fielding a military is the one place that still asks `has_base()` directly.

## Audit (from code)

`ScenarioUnitLogic._field` (behind `can_field` and `can_field_structurally`, and so every counter,
defeat and siege rule) is `Or(trained tiers) & has_base() & can_sustain(every resource the tiers
cost)`; the paid form also asks `ages.can_pay_for(unit.age)`. Moving to the price means dropping the
direct `& has_base()`.

What that would change (checked 2026-10-22):

- **Food-only units** (Scout Cavalry, 80 food) reach the base only through easy food, which asks for
  a base only once #05 (with #13's raw-food change) lands. **Do not do this before #05.**
- **Gold-only units** (the Monk, 100 gold): Attila 3 opens in the Castle Age and its gold lumps are
  an authored easy gold source with no base, so the Monk would be fielded there off the lumps with a
  Monastery and no Villager Line or villager food. Arguably right (monks come from the Monastery),
  but it is a change in what logic accepts.
- Every other fieldable unit costs a gathered resource, so the price already brings the base. The
  cost-free units in the data (Antiquity units) are not trainable in any shipped scenario (probed
  with Attila, Joan and Genghis enabled).

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-22 after grilling.

**Category:** enhancement
**Blocked by:** #05 and #17. Start only once both are done; their base, raw-food and paid-building
changes are assumed here.
**Summary:** Fielding a unit and defeating a player reach the base only through the price, never by
asking `has_base()` directly

**Current behavior:**
`ScenarioUnitLogic._field`, behind both `can_field` (paid) and `can_field_structurally`, is the
trained tiers, `& scenario.has_base()`, and `& economy.can_sustain(every resource the tiers cost)`.
Every military question (roles, counters, siege, navy, `has_military`) ends there. On top of that,
the per-scenario `defeat_rules` in the Attila, Genghis and Joan starting states ask
`scenario_logic.has_base() & military.<...>` in about 40 places; those are redundant today because
the military question already asks the base. ADR-0002 says no location or prerequisite asks for a
base itself: it reaches the base only through its price.

**Desired behavior:**
- `_field` asks no base, in both forms: the trained tiers (with `ages.can_pay_for` and, after #17,
  the paid training building, in the paid form) and `can_sustain` of their cost. No `paid` branch
  around the base, since neither form has one.
- No `defeat_rules` asks `has_base()` directly. Each such rule keeps the rest of its conjunction
  unchanged (`military.counters_building()`, `has_siege()`, and so on).
- A unit therefore needs a base exactly when some resource in its price (including, after #17, its
  training building's price) has no source that reaches no base. That is deliberate: do not add a
  base back for any unit line (the faith line included).
- Leave the structural form's `can_sustain` to #19, which removes it (folded in from #21). If #19
  has landed first, the structural form already asks no price; keep it that way.
- Respect ADR-0002: add no direct `has_base()` anywhere.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ScenarioUnitLogic._field`, `can_field`, `can_field_structurally`.
- `ScenarioStartingState.defeat_rules` overrides in the Attila, Genghis and Joan scenario logic.
- `ScenarioMilitaryLogic._protecting_boats` (the structural form's one caller): behaviour inside the
  easy sources should not change, because those sources ask the base themselves after #05.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `_field` contains no `has_base()` in either form.
- [ ] No `defeat_rules` override asks `has_base()`. `grep -rn "has_base()"` over `logic/` and `rules/`
      finds only the gathering side (easy gold and stone, chopping wood) and `ScenarioLogic`'s own
      definition; `generation/LocalStart.py` is unchanged.
- [ ] A rule test pins the shipped Attila 3 Monk: with every item except the Villager Line (and the
      professions it implies, where shuffled), state whether the Monk is fieldable in the Castle Age,
      assert that, and record the answer and the reason (which source made it true or false) in
      this issue. Do not change scenario data to make it come out either way.
- [ ] A rule test shows a food-costing unit (Scout Cavalry or Militia) is not fieldable where no
      base is reachable, so the base still arrives through the price.
- [ ] Every economy question still terminates with `market_trade` on and off, and with partner trade
      (the existing termination tests pass).
- [ ] `AGEIPELAGO_PATH=<Ageipelago> python -m pytest worlds/age2de/test` passes. Where an existing

- [ ] Before/after replay counts recorded in this issue.
