# 18: Military asks for a base directly instead of through its price

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split from [#05](05-attila1-forging-without-town-center.md) during triage

**Status:** needs-triage

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

During #05's grilling the maintainer ruled that a base should reach locations through their price
(easy sources), not as a direct requirement. Ages, techs, units and buildings follow that once #05
lands. Fielding a military is the one place that still asks `has_base()` directly.

## Audit (from code)

`ScenarioUnitLogic._field` (behind `can_field` and `can_field_structurally`, and so every counter,
defeat and siege rule) is `Or(trained tiers) & has_base() & can_sustain(every resource the tiers
cost)`; the paid form also asks `ages.can_pay_for(unit.age)`. Moving to the price means dropping the
direct `& has_base()`.

What that would change (checked 2026-10-22):

- **Food-only units** (Scout Cavalry, 80 food) reach the base only through easy food, which asks for
  a base only once #05 (with #13's raw-food change) lands. **Do not do this before #05.**
- **Gold-only units** (the Monk, 100 gold): Attila 3 opens in the Castle Age and its gold lumps are
  an authored easy gold source with no base, so the Monk would be fielded there off the lumps with a
  Monastery and no Villager Line or villager food. Arguably right (monks come from the Monastery),
  but it is a change in what logic accepts.
- Every other fieldable unit costs a gathered resource, so the price already brings the base. The
  cost-free units in the data (Antiquity units) are not trainable in any shipped scenario (probed
  with Attila, Joan and Genghis enabled).
