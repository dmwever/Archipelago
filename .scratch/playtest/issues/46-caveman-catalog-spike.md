## Question

#16 asks what happens to a scenario-granted Transport Ship and Trade Cart under caveman, and
proposed answering it with a seed and a playthrough. Two rows is the wrong unit of work: the
same code path runs over **every** row of the seed's unit table, the outcome differs by row, and
the harness that can answer it for two rows can answer it for all of them in the same run.

So: for every row in the installed seed's unit table, what does `ApplyCaveman` actually do —
what does the row become, does the replacement get created at all, and where does it end up?

## What the code says, before the run

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* From `Caveman.xs` and `Unitsanity.xs` as of
> Ageipelago `a7b5534`, and from `empires2_x2_p1.dat` read with `kit dat`.

- `cavemanTarget` (`Caveman.xs:151`) walks the row's line for the highest tier **strictly below**
  the row's own that passes `technologyUnlocked`, and falls back to `CAVEMAN_MILITIA` (74) when
  there is none. `technologyUnlocked` (`:136`) returns false for any `locked` row, so at the
  opening of a caveman seed — nothing granted, every row locked — **every non-exempt row targets
  Militia**, including every tier-0 row, which has nothing below it to find.
- `ApplyCaveman` (`:210`) spares only four cases: a ledger entry of `CAVEMAN_REMOVED`, the immune
  list (`MercenarySpawn.xs:77`), the row's `cavemanExempt` flag, and `ExemptUnitTypeFromCaveman`
  (only the Flemish Militia trap uses it).
- `is_caveman_exempt` (`client/handlers/install/UnitData.py:71`) is `unit.line in VILLAGER_LINES`.
- `transformUnit` (`:186`) removes the unit, writes `CAVEMAN_REMOVED` to the ledger, and **then**
  calls `xsCreateUnit(target, 1, position, false, true, false)`. If that create fails it returns
  early. The unit is gone and its ledger row is closed, so the unit item arriving later restores
  nothing.

### The catalog, from the .dat

The installed seed's `UnitData.xs` has **107 rows** across 37 lines, 40 of them `cavemanExempt`.
Joining those ids against `empires2_x2_p1.dat` (`kit dat sql`, then `kit dat terrain-restrictions`)
gives nine distinct `terrain_table_id` values, which collapse into three groups whose passability
rows are identical to each other:

| place | terrain tables | rows | exempt | what |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `foot` | 7, 28 | 74 | 37 | infantry, archers, monks, cavalry, villagers, foot and mounted heroes |
| `siege` | 20, 29, 31 | 16 | 3 | siege, the Trade Cart, the Bombard Cannon, the packed Trebuchet |
| `water` | 3, 13, 15, 30 | 17 | **0** | every ship |

Two things follow that #16 could not see from two rows:

1. **No water row is exempt.** All 17 ships are cavemanifiable, not just the Transport Ship.
2. **Militia (74) is terrain table 7.** A ship's replacement is a land unit asked for at the
   ship's water position. Whether the engine accepts that with `checkCollision = false` is the
   single question the whole water half turns on, and it is not recorded anywhere in the KB.

### Lines with no fallback

14 lines in the catalog have exactly one tier, so a locked row there goes straight to Militia
with no intermediate step:

| place | rows |
| --- | --- |
| `foot` | Hand Cannoneer (5), Monk (125), Petard (440), Elite Konnik on foot (1236) |
| `siege` | Bombard Cannon (36), **Trade Cart (128)**, packed Trebuchet (331), Siege Tower (1105), Rocket Cart (1904) |
| `water` | Fishing Ship (13), Trade Cog (17), Cannon Galleon (420), **Transport Ship (545)**, Dromon (1795) |

Both rows #16 names are here. The four multi-tier water lines (833 galleys, 857 fire ships, 858
demolition ships, 934 hulks) fall back within the line to a tier-0 **ship** — but only once that
tier-0 row is unlocked, which at the start of a caveman seed it is not.

## The harness

The script is **`Test.xs` in the Ageipelago xs folder** — the live file the scenario runs by
name, with no staged copy beside it to run by mistake. `spikes/46/` holds the map recipe, the
ledger, the decoder and the catalog. See [its README](../spikes/46/README.md) for the full
mechanics. In short:

- `Test.xs` drives the real `UnitsanityChecks` rule with no client and no seeded scenario, the
  same way the #10 harness does, and walks every row of `unitTableCount` one at a time: read the
  row, record what `cavemanTarget` predicts, create the unit for P1, give it 4 ticks, record what
  is standing, remove it.
- Each row lands in one of four outcomes: `left alone`, `swapped`, **`destroyed`** (the ledger
  says `CAVEMAN_REMOVED` and nothing replaced it), or `untested` (the engine refused both spots).
- It finds its land and water spots by **creating a probe unit and seeing whether the engine
  accepts it**, not by reading terrain, so no terrain-id table has to be correct for the run to
  be sound.
- `XsTestingWater.aoe2scenario` is XsTesting with tiles 90-119 on both axes set to Water, Medium,
  built from XsTesting with `kit scen patch` and checked with `kit scen write-check`
  (`map_tiles_changed: 900`, `rebuild_ok: true`, `invariant_ok: true`, XS wiring unchanged). It
  replaces #35's `TS_Spike.aoe2scenario`, which exists only on the maintainer's disk.
- `./xs-check.exe -I . -- Test.xs` reports **0 errors** on the script as written.

## Run 1 (2026-10-28)

> *This was generated by AI during triage,* from `spikes/46/run1-void.xsdat`, which passed 19206
> of 19206 ledger checks with its footer intact.

**Answered, and these stand:**

- **107 of 107 rows measured.** 66 swapped, 41 left alone, none destroyed, none untested.
- **Every non-exempt row targets Militia at a fully locked table**, as `cavemanTarget` predicts.
  The 41 left alone are the 40 `cavemanExempt` rows plus Militia itself, which targets `self`.
  By class: foot 36 swapped / 38 left alone, siege 13 / 3, water 17 / 0.
- **No ship is exempt.** All 17 water rows swapped. Question 1 is settled, question 4 is settled
  (the 40 exempt rows held, including the 3 exempt siege rows), question 5 is settled (nothing
  untested).

**Void for water, and the fault is the harness's:** map positions were built as
`xsVectorSet(x, 0.0, z)`, but this codebase puts the map axes in **x and y** with the third slot
unused (`APavilion.xs:38`, `TrapItems.xs:414`). Every row, ships included, was created at row 0
of the map, on grass. So the 17 water rows record a ship on land becoming a Militia on land, and
questions 2 and 3 are untouched.

**One new fact worth keeping:** `xsCreateUnit` with `checkCollision = false` **ignores terrain**.
The run created a Transport Ship on grass at (2, 0). That is why run 1's land-first fallback
never reached the water spot, and it is also why `transformUnit`'s Militia may well appear on
open water rather than failing — which would make the outcome "a Militia floating where the
transport was", not destruction. Run 2 decides it.

## Run 2 (2026-10-28): the answer

> *This was generated by AI during triage,* from `spikes/46/run2.xsdat`, which passed 19206 of
> 19206 ledger checks with its footer intact. 1065 records, 107 of 107 rows, each probed twice.

The run had real water: the `STAGE` lines report the land spot (60, 60) at terrain 0 and the
water spot **(96, 96) at terrain 23**, the Water, Medium the map paints at 90-119.

**Ships are knocked down, not destroyed.** All 17 water rows, standing on water, swapped to a
Militia **created at (96, 96)** — on the water tile itself. `transformUnit`'s `xsCreateUnit`
never failed, for any row, on either spot.

| | on land | on water |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `foot` | 36 swapped / 38 left alone | 36 / 38 |
| `siege` | 13 / 3 | 13 / 3 |
| `water` | 17 / 0 | **17 / 0** |

- **Question 1** — no non-exempt row survives; all 66 swap, on both spots.
- **Question 2** — a ship becomes a Militia standing on open water. Not destroyed.
- **Question 3** — the replacement lands on the probe's own tile every time, on water as on
  land. The engine does not relocate it.
- **Question 4** — all 40 exempt rows held, on both spots.
- **Question 5** — nothing untested; `xsCreateUnit` with `checkCollision = false` accepts any
  unit on any terrain, which is also why the land and water columns are identical.

**The movement question, closed without a run.** Whether that Militia can move off the water was
the one thing run 2 did not cover. **The maintainer settled it from play on 2026-10-28: the
Militia are stuck, and that is acceptable.** Not measured by this spike — recorded here as the
maintainer's ruling. A movement probe was drafted and dropped.

So a granted ship under caveman is, for the player, gone: what remains is a Militia that cannot
leave the water. The ledger entry survives, so the unit item still restores the line, but
nothing the player can use arrives until it does.

## Questions

1. **Does any non-exempt row survive?** The prediction is that all 67 non-exempt rows target
   Militia at a fully locked table. A row that comes back `left alone` contradicts the reading of
   `cavemanTarget` and needs explaining.
2. **What happens to a ship?** For each of the 17 water rows: `swapped` with a Militia standing
   on water, `swapped` with it somewhere else, or `destroyed`. This is #16's question, widened.
3. **Where does a replacement land?** `PLACE` records the position of both the probe and the
   replacement. A replacement that is not at the probe's tile means the engine relocated it, and
   that is worth knowing for the garrison work in [#41](41-cavemanify-garrisoned-units.md) too.
4. **Do the exempt rows hold?** All 40 should come back `left alone`. The 3 exempt `siege` rows
   (the Flemish-Militia cart 1338, and heroes 644 and 650) are the ones worth a second look.
5. **Is anything `untested`?** A row the engine refuses on both land and water is a row the
   harness cannot speak for, and the report should say so rather than imply coverage.

## What the answers settle

- **Ships are destroyed:** game-side bug, and a bigger one than #16 assumed. The fix is to widen
  `UnitData.is_caveman_exempt` beyond villager lines, or to make `transformUnit` create first and
  remove second so a failed create leaves the original standing.
- **Ships become Militia on the water:** logic bug. The #01 rows for Transport Ship, Trade Cart
  and French Army take the same `survives_caveman` gate as the monk fix, and the scenario-objective
  rows listed in #16's 2026-10-28 triage notes take it with them.
- **Ships are left alone:** there is a mechanism the code reading missed. Find it and record it in
  `CONTEXT.md` before changing any logic.
