## Report

> *This was generated by AI during review.*

The land tier gate for `Tier.OPEN` is `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_defend_resources()` →
`ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified()`: a strongpoint (Town Center, Castle, Fortified Church, Krepost,
Donjon), or a wall (Stone Wall, Palisade Wall) together with a tower (Watch Tower, Bombard Tower).
Each one is asked through `has_building_structurally`, "standing or buildable", so a buildable
defence costs nothing. `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_hold_a_shoreline` asks `is_fortified()`, so the
shoreline gate inherits the same free defence.

The navy guard on the water gates is paid for, since #19 made it a route need. The land guard is
not, and that asymmetry is the only reason `tier_gates` takes a `boat_guard` parameter while land
gates take none.

Maintainer's direction (2026-10-23): "There's no such thing as a completely free defense." And:
"Logically harder is usually in-game easier."

## Proposed shape

> *This was generated by AI during review. Not yet grilled.*

- A defence that is **standing** stays free: it is on the map already.
- A **buildable** defence keeps its structural part in the leaf, and its build cost becomes route
  needs of every route working through an open-tier gate. Group the defences by cost the way
  `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.boat_guards()` groups navy lines. Each group adds a guarded copy of the
  route. The unguarded copy keeps the answer for when no defence is paid for.
- `tier_gates` then takes one guard for every kind of gate (land, shoreline, afloat), and the
  boat-only parameter becomes general.
- Cycles such as open-ground stone → a stone wall → stone are what the #19 resolver is for: that
  stone has to come from a route that is already running (base-tier stone, cogs, the market).

## Comments

### Triage Notes (2026-10-23)

- **Verified from code.** `_open_gate("land")` -> `can_defend_resources()` -> `_fortified()`: each
  defence asked through `has_building_structurally`, whose buildable half is `ScenarioCanBuild`
  with no cost. `can_hold_a_shoreline` asks `is_fortified()`, `can_build_multiple_tc()` or a Dock,
  all free. ADR-0002 rules fortification and dropsites pay for nothing, so this ticket amends it.
- **Narrower than reported.** Every route at scale already holds a base, whose Town Center is paid.
  The free defence bites on base-free routes: villagers of *some* on open ground, relics, shore
  jobs of *some*. With a Dock, the shoreline gate is already open for Fishing Ships.

### Grilling (2026-10-23)

- **A Dock still holds a shoreline**: it protects fishing boats and defends. A buildable Dock's cost
  becomes route needs like any other defence.
- **Strength: *some*.** A defence is a one-time build, as the naval guard (#21).
- **No bank pays for a defence**: its resources come only from a running route, as the naval guard.
- **The first Town Center is paid as ADR-0002 says** (standing, or the Town Center items). It is not
  a strongpoint: a Town Center defends open ground only as a **second** one, through
  `can_build_multiple_tc()`.
- **`can_build_multiple_tc()` costs *some* wood and *some* stone in every case**: the open-ground
  strongpoint, the shoreline's "more Town Centers", and the camp's "or more Town Centers" (#22).
- **A standing Town Center does not defend open ground**: it is the base's. Only built extra Town
  Centers count.
- **A standing defence opens no gate.** Resources a standing Castle, tower, wall or Dock guards are
  already summed into `Tier.BASE`; the gates ask only defences P1 builds and pays for. The same for
  every way to hold a shoreline (standing Dock, Mill with a fortification, more Town Centers). A
  mis-tiered shore is a `ScenarioResources.py` data fix.
- **Bundle or two needs: moot.** Route needs are (resource, strength) pairs, so a wall and a tower
  need the same resources at *some* either way. Grouping by cost is the agent's choice.
- **Lands on its own**, after #25 and before #26 and #27, with #22's fill gate: if the failure
  count rises, stop and hand back; loosen nothing.
- **ADR-0002 amended in place** (fortification and dock leave the pays-for-nothing list; a defence
  consequence beside the naval guard's). `CONTEXT.md` gains **Defence**.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-23 after grilling.

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Price every defence an economy route works behind as route needs of *some*, and stop standing defences from opening any gate

**Current behavior:**
`ScenarioResourceLogic.tier_gates(kind, boat_guard)` gates each map area (`Tier`). For
`Tier.OPEN`, the land gate is `can_defend_resources()`, which accepts a strongpoint (Town Center,
Castle, Fortified Church, Krepost, Donjon) or a wall (Stone Wall, Palisade Wall) with a tower
(Watch Tower, Bombard Tower), each asked by `has_building_structurally`: standing or buildable,
never paid. The shoreline gate is `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_hold_a_shoreline()` (a Mill with that
same defence, `can_build_multiple_tc()`, or a Dock structurally) or the naval guard. Only the naval
guard is priced: `_guarded_routes` adds a "... with Guarded Boats" copy of a route per cost group
of `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.can_guard_boats()`, its costs as route needs of *some*.
`can_build_multiple_tc()` (Castle Age, builders, the Town Center's items) costs nothing, and the
camps (`has_*_camp()`, "or more Town Centers") ask it too.

**Desired behavior:** see **Defence** in `CONTEXT.md` and ADR-0002's defence consequence (both
already updated).
- **A standing defence opens no gate.** What it guards is already in `Tier.BASE`. No gate asks
  `starts_with_building` for a defence: not a standing Castle, wall, tower, Dock or Town Center.
- **The base's Town Center is no strongpoint.** A Town Center defends only as a second one:
  `can_build_multiple_tc()`.
- **Open ground (land gate)** holds through a built defence: more Town Centers, a Castle (or a
  unique strongpoint where the civilisation can build one), or a wall with a tower. Each is asked
  structurally (`can_build_structurally`, never `starts_with_building`), and its build cost becomes
  route needs of *some* of the route working through the gate.
- **Shoreline gate** holds through a built Dock (its cost as needs of *some*), a Mill with a built
  land defence (the Mill stays structural and free: a dropsite), more Town Centers, or the naval
  guard. A standing Dock, Mill or Town Center opens nothing on its own.
- **Afloat gate** unchanged: the naval guard only.
- **More Town Centers cost *some* wood and *some* stone wherever they are asked**: the land gate,
  the shoreline gate, and the camps' "or more Town Centers" inside routes at scale.
- **No bank pays for a defence**: route needs only, never `HasResourceAmount`.
- **Costs come from the building data** (`Age2BuildingData.cost`), not hand-written amounts; at
  *some* only which resources a defence costs matters. Group the defences by that resource set, as
  `can_guard_boats()` groups navy lines. Each group adds a guarded copy of the route; the unguarded
  copy keeps the answer with no defence (`Tier.BASE` and whatever needs no gate).
- **`tier_gates` takes one guard for every kind of gate** (land, shoreline, afloat); the boat-only
  parameter becomes general. A route behind the shoreline gate may take a copy per way in
  (land-defence group, Dock, more Town Centers, boat-guard group): an Or between ways with
  different needs is one route per way.
- **Every caller of the land gate** prices it: villager routes of *some* and at scale, relics, the
  summed villager-and-boat routes. Herd gates (open ground free) and the enemy gate
  (`must_steal_base`) are unchanged.
- **Cycles are the resolver's.** A route never seeds itself, so a route of *some* mining stone
  only on open ground cannot pay for its own wall; that stone must come from another running route.
- `/explain` names the guarded copies the way it names "... with Guarded Boats".

**Key interfaces:**
- `ScenarioResourceLogic.tier_gates`, `_open_gate`, `can_defend_resources`, `_guarded_routes`: one
  general guard, and guarded copies per defence cost group.
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified`, `is_fortified`, `can_hold_a_shoreline`,
  `can_build_multiple_tc`, `has_*_camp`: structural only; their costs move out into route needs.
- `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.can_guard_boats()`: the shape to follow for defence cost groups.
- `EconomyRoute` (`leaf`, `needs`): unchanged.

**Acceptance criteria:** use the `test_economy_catalog.py` toy scenario (every leaf fact a
switch). Every criterion below must go red with the fix reverted unless marked *held before*;
record the evidence under each.
- [ ] Open ground, no base: villagers mining gold on `Tier.OPEN` behind a buildable wall and tower
      give *some* gold when the defence's resources come from routes of *some* (a few lumberjacks,
      base-tier stone), and not when those resources are only banked.
- [ ] A route cannot pay for its own defence: stone only on open ground, no other stone route, a
      buildable wall and tower: no *some* stone.
- [ ] A standing defence opens no gate: a standing Castle (or wall and tower) with nothing
      buildable adds nothing from `Tier.OPEN`.
- [ ] The base's Town Center is no strongpoint: a base and nothing else buildable count nothing
      from `Tier.OPEN` towards easy gold.
- [ ] More Town Centers: with `can_build_multiple_tc()` holding structurally, the land gate, the
      shoreline gate and a camp's "or more Town Centers" (a hunt term with no Mill, Folwark or Mule
      Cart) each hold only with *some* wood and *some* stone running; with either missing, none
      does.
- [ ] Shoreline: villagers work shore fish with no base through a built Dock only with *some* wood

- [ ] *Held before:* Fishing Ships with wood at scale and a buildable Dock still work shore fish
