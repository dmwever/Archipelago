## Report

> *This was generated by AI during review.*

The land tier gate for `Tier.OPEN` is `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_defend_resources()` →
`ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified()`: a strongpoint (Town Center, Castle, Fortified Church, Krepost,
Donjon), or a wall (Stone Wall, Palisade Wall) together with a tower (Watch Tower, Bombard Tower).
Each one is asked through `has_building_structurally`, "standing or buildable", so a buildable
defence costs nothing. `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_hold_a_shoreline` asks `is_fortified()`, so the
shoreline gate inherits the same free defence.

The navy guard on the water gates is paid for, since #19 made it a route need. The land guard is
not, and that asymmetry is the only reason `tier_gates` takes a `boat_guard` parameter while land
gates take none.

Maintainer's direction (2026-10-23): "There's no such thing as a completely free defense." And:
"Logically harder is usually in-game easier."

## Proposed shape

> *This was generated by AI during review.* **Superseded** - this shape was built on
> 2026-10-23 and thrown away; see **Grilling (2026-10-25)** and the Agent Brief.

- A defence that is **standing** stays free: it is on the map already.
- A **buildable** defence keeps its structural part in the leaf, and its build cost becomes route
  needs of every route working through an open-tier gate. Group the defences by cost the way
  `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.boat_guards()` groups navy lines. Each group adds a guarded copy of the
  route. The unguarded copy keeps the answer for when no defence is paid for.
- `tier_gates` then takes one guard for every kind of gate (land, shoreline, afloat), and the
  boat-only parameter becomes general.
- Cycles such as open-ground stone → a stone wall → stone are what the #19 resolver is for: that
  stone has to come from a route that is already running (base-tier stone, cogs, the market).

### Grilling (2026-10-23)

- **A Dock still holds a shoreline**: it protects fishing boats and defends. A buildable Dock's cost
  becomes route needs like any other defence.
- **Strength: *some*.** A defence is a one-time build, as the naval guard (#21).
- **No bank pays for a defence**: its resources come only from a running route, as the naval guard.
- **The first Town Center is paid as ADR-0002 says** (standing, or the Town Center items). It is not

- **A standing Town Center does not defend open ground**: it is the base's. Only built extra Town

- **A standing defence opens no gate.** Resources a standing Castle, tower, wall or Dock guards are

  mis-tiered shore is a `ScenarioResources.py` data fix.
- **Bundle or two needs: moot.** Route needs are (resource, strength) pairs, so a wall and a tower

### Grilling (2026-10-25): the first design was built and thrown away

> *This was generated by AI after implementing the 2026-10-23 brief, having it rejected, and
> reverting it.* Recorded so the discarded shape is not proposed again.

**What was built.** `tier_gates(kind, guard)` took a `Guard` value object; every defence was
grouped by the `frozenset[Resource]` it cost; each economy route was copied once per cost bundle
("Gold Miners with a Guard of Wood and Stone") with that bundle as `EconomyRoute.needs`; and the
guard was threaded through fourteen new method signatures to reach the leaves. The catalog went
from 42 routes per scenario to 85.

**Why it was rejected.** The maintainer's words: the guard parameters were being puked all over
the codebase, and `boat_guard` replacing the old `can_guard_boats()` function had been unwelcome
in #19 and #21 too. The route multiplication, the cost groups and the generated route names all
followed from one decision - that a defence is paid in route needs - and that decision was wrong.

**What it also broke.** Pricing the land gate for routes of *some* took stone away from Attila 2,
3, 6 and Joan 2 outright: those four hold stone only on open ground, every land defence costs
stone, and no route seeds itself, so nothing could ever pay for the first wall.
`test_determinism.py::TestCampaignOrder` went red - with Attila and Joan enabled and **every item
collected**, The Fall of Rome was unwinnable. The 100-seed fill gate passed anyway, which is why
`TestCampaignOrder` is now a criterion in its own right.

**The reversals.**

- *Route needs are the currency for a defence* (2026-10-23 grilling) -> **no.** A defence pays for
  itself in the expression that asks for it. `EconomyRoute.needs` keeps its six real users.
- *`tier_gates` takes one guard for every kind of gate* -> **no.** The gates take no parameter at
  all.
- *More Town Centers cost some wood and some stone wherever they are asked, camps included* ->
  still true, but it is paid inside `can_build_multiple_tc`, so no caller carries it.
- *#19 and #21's `boat_guard` and "... with Guarded Boats" copies* -> **unwound here.**

**What held.** A standing defence opens no gate; the base's own Town Center is no strongpoint; a
bank never pays; costs come from `Age2BuildingData.cost`; a defence is asked at *some*.

**The idea that resolved it**, and the maintainer's: *only ground worked at scale has to be held.*
A route of *some* walks out and comes home, so it needs no defence; a route at scale does. Stone
of *some* off open ground is therefore free and buys the wall that holds the same ground at scale.
The cycle that made route needs necessary never forms, and the four scenarios keep their stone.

**One constraint the next agent must not rediscover the hard way.** A gate cannot ask the economy
it sits inside. `_fortified` asking `has_some_source` raises
`RecursionError: ('ScenarioHasEconomyRoutes', AP_ATTILA_1) asks itself` - verified, not theorised.
That is what the ungated route layer is for, and why ADR-0003 now records it as the one place its
rejected "authored layers" option is used.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage, rewritten 2026-10-25 after the second grilling.*
> The first design of this ticket was built and thrown away; see **Grilling (2026-10-25)** below
> for what was reversed and why. This brief is the contract - read it, not the Proposed shape.

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Only ground worked *at scale* has to be held, and a defence pays for itself where it
is asked

**Current behavior:**
`ScenarioResourceLogic.tier_gates(kind, boat_guard)` gates each map area (`Tier`) for every route,
of *some* and at scale alike. The land gate is `can_defend_resources()` ->
`ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified()`: a strongpoint (Town Center, Castle, Fortified Church,
Krepost, Donjon) or a wall (Stone Wall, Palisade Wall) with a tower (Watch Tower, Bombard Tower),
each asked by `has_building_structurally` - standing or buildable, never paid. The shoreline gate
is `can_hold_a_shoreline()` or the naval guard. Only the naval guard is priced, and it is priced by
multiplying routes: `_guarded_routes` adds a "... with Guarded Boats" copy per cost group of
`can_guard_boats()`, with that group's costs as `EconomyRoute.needs`, and threads a `boat_guard`
rule down through eleven term builders to reach the gates.

**Desired behavior:** see **Defence** in `CONTEXT.md`, ADR-0002's defence consequences and
ADR-0003's last two consequences (all three already rewritten to match this brief).

- **Open ground is free at *some*, and gated only at scale.** A route of *some* walks a few
  villagers out, takes what it needs and comes home; a route at scale has to hold the ground.
  Routes of *some* ask a free-open gate - `some_resource_gates()`, which is what `herd_gates()`
  becomes once sheep stop being a special case - and at-scale routes ask `tier_gates()`.
- **Neither gate takes a parameter.** No `boat_guard`, no guard object, no `through`, nothing
  threaded through the term builders.
- **A defence pays for itself where it is asked**, in the same expression that asks whether P1 can
  build it: `can_build_structurally(B.CASTLE) & can_pay_some(B.CASTLE.cost)`. Per building, per
  wall-and-tower pair, per navy line - each asks for *some* of what it actually costs, read off
  `Age2BuildingData.cost`, never a hand-written amount and never a cost group.
- **`can_pay_some(costs)` mirrors `can_pay`, one strength down**: `And(has_some_source(r) for each
  resource the build costs)`.
- **`has_some_source` resolves against the ungated routes, not the whole economy.** A gate cannot
  ask the economy it sits inside - it raises `RecursionError` from `ScenarioQuestion`, which is the
  reason route needs existed. The ungated set is every route whose leaf reads no gate: the routes
  of *some*, Farmers, Fish Traps, trade, the market, and what a scenario grants. Build it as its
  own composition of the route builders (`ungated_routes()` beside `gated_routes()`, with Farmers
  and Fish Traps moved out of `villager_routes_at_scale`/`boat_routes` into a `renewable_routes()`),
  so moving a route between groups is the only edit needed. Memoise it behind its own
  `ScenarioQuestion`, as `ScenarioHasEconomyRoutes` is.
- **A standing defence opens no gate.** Each way is asked with `can_build_structurally`, never
  `starts_with_building`. Genghis 6 is the only shipped scenario that starts with a real defence
  and its Castle guards none of its open-tier resources, so this costs nothing today.
- **The second Town Center pays inside `can_build_multiple_tc`** - alongside the Castle Age and the
  item requirements, in `_multiple_tc()` - so the strongpoint, the shoreline and the camps' "or
  more Town Centers" all inherit the payment with no parameter and no caller changes.
- **The naval guard goes back to a plain rule.** `can_guard_boats()` returns a `Rule` again, fields
  each line structurally and pays `can_pay_some` of that line's own cost, so a cheap line is not
  taxed at a dear one's price. `boat_guard`, `_guarded_routes` and every generated "... with
  Guarded Boats" route name go.
- **`EconomyRoute.needs` stays** for the six routes that genuinely use it: Farmers, Fishing Ships
  (food and gold), Fish Traps and Market Trade want wood at scale; Trade Cogs wants some wood and
  some gold; Trade Carts wants wood at scale and some gold. Only the guard-derived needs go.
- **The route catalog does not grow.** 42 routes stay 42. No copies, no cost bundles, no generated
  names in `/explain`.
- **Open-tier gold at scale still asks for a base** as well as a defence; `has_base()` on the
  at-scale villager routes is untouched.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ScenarioResourceLogic.tier_gates`, `some_resource_gates` (was `herd_gates`), `_open_gate`,
  `can_defend_resources`, `can_pay_some`, `ungated_routes`, `gated_routes`, `renewable_routes`.
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified`, `is_fortified`, `can_hold_a_shoreline`, `_multiple_tc`: pay
  inline; signatures unchanged.
- `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.can_guard_boats()`: back to `-> Rule`.
- `ScenarioQuestions`: `ScenarioHasSomeResource` answers from the ungated economy; a new sibling of
  `ScenarioHasEconomyRoutes` memoises it.
- `EconomyRoute`, `_guarded_routes`: the latter deleted, the former unchanged.
