# 15: Joan 2 "Defeat Burgundians at the Bridge" accepts a military that needs Orleans first

**Reported:** 2026-10-20, playtesting

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Report

The Maid of Orleans: Defeat Burgundians at the Bridge can be completed in logic with just
`has_military`, but `has_military` needs the player to have reached Orleans first.

## Audit (from code)

Rule (`rules/joan_rules/joan_2.py:16-19, 31`):

```
can_cross          = HasAny(Trade Carts, Dock)
can_conquer_bridge = Has(Trade Carts) | has_military()
```

`has_military()` → `can_field` → `has_base()` and buildings to train from. In Joan 2 every one of
those comes from the **Orleans item alone** (`logic/joan/joan_2.py:32-42`): `has_base`, `has_vils`,
and the standing Town Center, Mill, Archery Range, Barracks, Stable, Blacksmith and Market are all
`has_orleans = Has(Orleans)`.

The Orleans item only gives the player that base once they get there. Getting there is the crossing
(`can_cross`, which is also the rule for Bring Joan to Orleans). Logic doesn't model the trip, so:

- the bridge is in logic with Orleans + an army, with no crossing;
- more broadly, **everything Joan 2 builds on the Orleans base** (economy, military, the castle
  objectives, Victory) is in logic without `can_cross`.

## Likely shape of a fix

Make the Orleans state `Has(Orleans) & can_cross` in `Joan2StartingState`, so the base, villagers
and buildings all wait for the crossing. Then decide what the bridge's military route should be:

- the army reachable after crossing, which may be circular if the bridge *is* the crossing; or
- the starting force (Joan 2 opens with Knights, a Two-Handed Swordsman and Scout Cavalry,
  `ScenarioStartupUnits.py`).

The reporter knows the map's geography better than the code does.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-22.

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** In Joan 2, the Orleans base must wait for the crossing, and the bridge's army route must arrive by Dock

**Current behavior:**
`Joan2StartingState` treats the Orleans scenario item as the whole requirement for the Orleans base:
`has_base`, `has_vils`, the standing Town Center, Mill, Archery Range, Barracks, Stable, Blacksmith
and Market, and the villager units all read `Has(Orleans)`. In game the item only pays out once Joan
reaches Orleans, which needs the crossing (Trade Carts' escort taking the bridge, or the Dock's
transports). So the base, the economy, the army built from it, the castle objectives, the English
defeats and Victory are all in logic with Orleans and no crossing.

`Joan2Rules` gives *Defeat Burgundians at the Bridge* the rule `Has(Trade Carts) | has_military()`.
The army route is only real for a player who crossed by Dock, but the rule doesn't say so.

**Desired behavior:**
- The Orleans state in `Joan2StartingState` is `Has(Orleans) & HasAny(Trade Carts, Dock)`. Every
  field that reads the Orleans item today reads this instead: `has_base`, `has_vils`, the seven
  `starts_with_building` entries, and the male and female villager `obtains_unit` entries. The
  Trade Carts' own units (Crossbowman, Knight, Trade Cart) and the Dock's Transport Ship keep their
  single-item rules.
- *Defeat Burgundians at the Bridge* is `Has(Trade Carts) | (Has(Dock) & has_military())`, still
  named "can take the bridge".
- *Bring Joan to Orleans* and *Find the Farming Village* keep `HasAny(Trade Carts, Dock)`.
- Everything downstream of `has_base` in Joan 2 (castle objectives, English and Burgundian defeat
  rules, Victory) gains the crossing through the starting state; their own rules don't change.

**Key interfaces:**
- `Joan2StartingState.__init__`: the Orleans rule and every field built on it.
- `Joan2Rules.set_rules`: the bridge rule.
- `ScenarioLogic.has_base()` / `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.has_military()`: unchanged; they inherit.

**Acceptance criteria** (Attila and Joan enabled, Attila starting, default options, as
`Age2RuleTestBase` builds):
- [ ] Without `Trade Carts` and `Dock`: *Defeat Burgundians at the Bridge*, *Victory* and
      *Complete The Maid of Orleans* are unreachable.
- [ ] Without `Trade Carts` only: the bridge and Victory are reachable.
- [ ] Without `Dock` and `Orleans`: the bridge is reachable (the escort route).
- [ ] Without `Orleans`: Victory is unreachable (unchanged).
- [ ] The full pool reaches every Joan 2 location.
- [ ] The bridge's item dependencies name both `Trade Carts` and `Dock`.
- [ ] Existing age2de tests pass (`AGEIPELAGO_PATH` set), and a fuzz run of the committed meta yaml
      shows no new fill failures.

**Out of scope:**
- Military asking for a base directly (#18); this fix holds either way.
- Rules for *Reach Blois*, *Find Dock for Crossing* and *Defeat Burgundians at Blois*.
- Counting the starting force as a military.
