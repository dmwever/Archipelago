# 54: Recruit Three Tribes is in logic when only two tribes can be reached

**Reported:** 2026-10-29, playtesting

**Status:** done (pending in-game test)

**Related:** [#51](51-in-game-checks.md) (the G1-1 row and its fix),
[#53](53-granted-units-need-access.md)

## Report

Get three tribes to join is in logic while, under caveman:

- Cyan (Uighur) is out of logic
- Grey (Ungirrad) is out of logic
- Purple (Tayichi'ud) and Green (Naiman) are mutually exclusive

So at most two tribes can be recruited, and `GEN1_RECRUIT_THREE_TRIBES` is reachable in logic
and not in game.

## Audit (from code, not reproduced in game)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The five Genghis 1 tribes and what is known about reaching each, from the #51 G1-1 session:

| tribe | colour | item | recruitable when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kereyid | blue | 4020 | from the start |
| Ungirrad | grey | 4013 | requires the Kereyids first |
| Tayichi'ud | purple | 4012 | requires killing troops |
| Naiman | green | 4014 | requires killing troops |
| Uighur | cyan | 4015 | requires killing Ornlu |

Under caveman the Uighurs are already gated by #51's fix:

```python
can_slay_ornlu: Rule = NOT_CAVEMAN | Has(Age2ItemData.AP_GENGHIS_1_KEREYID_TRIBE.item_name)
```

on `GEN1_RECRUIT_UIGHURS`. The report says the Uighurs are out of logic anyway, so whatever puts
them out is upstream of or additional to that gate.

The three counting locations are all `OBJECTIVE_BRANCHING_ANY`
(`locations/Locations.py:174-176`): `GEN1_RECRUIT_ONE_TRIBE` 30101,
`GEN1_RECRUIT_TWO_TRIBES` 30102, `GEN1_RECRUIT_THREE_TRIBES` 30103. They exist only under "any"
branching, which is the opposite arm from the per-tribe locations that `Genghis1Rules` gates
today - that file's tribe rules all sit inside `if self.world.pool.scenarios.all_scenario_branches:`.
So under "any" branching the counting locations appear to carry no rule at all.

What this needs before it can be fixed:

1. **Confirm the purple/green exclusivity in the scenario triggers.** The report says they are
   mutually exclusive; the mechanism is not yet read out of the `.aoe2scenario`. Whether it is a
   hard lock or an ordering is what decides whether the cap is two or three.
2. **Decide where recruitment order lives.** Nothing in `logic/genghis/genghis_1.py` models it
   today. Both this and #53 are the same gap seen from two directions, so they probably want one
   answer rather than two patches.
3. **Then gate the counting locations** on how many tribes are actually reachable, under "any"
   branching where they exist.

Severity: a location reachable in logic and not in game can strand a seed, so this is the more
serious of the two Genghis 1 reports. It is not newly introduced - the counting locations have
been unruled under "any" branching.

## Comments

### Fixed (2026-11-03), pending in-game test

`rules/genghis_rules/genghis_1.py`. Under "any" branching, Recruit Three Tribes is now gated:

```python
self.world.set_rule(self.locations[Age2ScenarioLocationData.GEN1_RECRUIT_THREE_TRIBES],
                    NOT_CAVEMAN | has_monk)
```

Grey needs blue, purple and green are mutually exclusive, and cyan needs more than the starting
cavemen, so under caveman the Kereyids are the gate. Without caveman the location stays free.
