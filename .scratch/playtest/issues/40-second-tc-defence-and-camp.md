**Summary:** Split a second Town Center into a defence rule and a camp rule, move "Lumberjacks with

**Current behavior**

`can_build_multiple_tc` is one rule. It asks the Town Center items (already spent on the base),
the Castle Age with nothing paid, and *some* of the Town Center's cost from the ungated routes.
It serves as a defence (`is_fortified`, `can_hold_a_shoreline`) and as a camp (`_camp_among`).
Because the camp choice reads the ungated routes, "Lumberjacks with a Base" is gated, so ungated
Farmers have no wood at scale in an ordinary base scenario. `can_hold_a_shoreline` asks a Mill with
nothing paid.

**Desired behavior**

- **Defence rule** (`tc_as_gate` or similar), used by `is_fortified` and `can_hold_a_shoreline`:
  *some* of the Town Center's cost and the Castle price, both read from the ungated routes. The
  Castle's food at scale is `EconomyRoutesYield` over the ungated routes at `AT_SCALE`.
- **Camp rule**, used by `_camp_among`: its own route version per at-scale route, with the Castle
  price and *some* wood and *some* stone as `needs`. It never reads the ungated routes.
- Neither rule asks the Town Center items.
- "Lumberjacks with a Base" moves from the gated to the ungated routes, and `test_defence_pays.py`
  pins it there.
- The Mill in `can_hold_a_shoreline` pays `can_pay_some(MILL.cost)`.
- Delete the remaining #37 allowlist entries. The list is empty when this ticket is done.
- Check that ADR-0002 and ADR-0003, amended during #32's triage, match what #38 to #40 built, and
  correct them where they do not.
