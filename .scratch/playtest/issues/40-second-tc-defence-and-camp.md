**The age's climb is capital too.** #42 ruled that reaching an age also pays for the climb into it:
a Town Center and the age-up requirement of the age before, each standing or paid for. Outside the
economy #42 builds it. Inside the economy, every route here that waits on an age the scenario
starts below also funds the climb, from outside the economy, beside the age price it already adds:

- A standing building is free.
- The Town Center is paid for by the Town Center items. They are seed items, so the route does not
  ask itself.
- An age-up building is paid for by its seed item where one is pooled (Lumber Camp, Dock, Market),
  or else by a `wood: some` need, as a Mill is funded. A building the route already stands on
  counts: a cog route's seeded Dock is one of Feudal's two.
- A Castle (an Imperial climb) is paid for by easy stone, `stone: at scale`. No route waits on
  Imperial today.
- An age the scenario starts in or past needs no climb.

#42 also widens the never-bare walk so an age costs its climb's resources. Expect this ticket's
allowlist lines to change text when #42 lands; #42 lists which.

**The Castle as a defence needs easy stone** (#42, maintainer). In the strongpoint branch of the
defence rule, the Castle asks easy stone read from the ungated routes at `AT_SCALE`, the same shape
as this ticket's ungated food at scale, in place of `can_pay_some`. The exception: the stone routes
at scale also accept a Castle paid for with *some* stone, as an Or on their own open-ground gate,
so stone still buys the Castle that holds it. The gate keeps no parameter. The Fortified Church,
Krepost and Donjon stay at *some*. The glossary's **Defence** and ADR-0002 carry this already.
