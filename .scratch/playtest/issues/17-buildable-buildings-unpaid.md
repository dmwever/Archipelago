# 17: Buildings "standing or buildable" are never paid for

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split from [#05](05-attila1-forging-without-town-center.md) during triage

**Status:** needs-triage

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The #05 trace shows the age-up "Any 2 of" buildings (Dock, Mill) and the Blacksmith as "standing or
buildable" in Attila 1 with no wood income at all. Reproduced in a rule test on 2026-10-22: with
every item except Bleda's Camp and the two Town Center items, `has_building` is True for the Dock
(150 wood), Mill (100 wood), Blacksmith (150 wood), Lumber Camp and Mining Camp (100 wood each).

## Audit (from code)

`ScenarioLogic.has_building` is `starts_with_building[building] | buildings.can_build_building(building)`.
The buildable half is structural: civilisation, scenario exclusions, and `ScenarioCanBuild` (build
permission plus builders). It never asks `economy.can_pay(building.cost)`.

Callers that inherit the unpaid building include the age-up requirement, the tech-building check,
every dropsite question (`has_wood_dropsite`, `has_food_dropsite`, ...), `is_fortified`, and
`can_hold_a_shoreline`. The Build locations themselves do pay (`Logic.can_build_location_anywhere`
asks `can_pay(building.cost)`), so only the rules that lean on a building are unpaid.

After #05 lands, ages need a base, which needs the Town Center items, so a scenario with no wood
income still has 275 banked wood from the Town Center item. That covers the Town Center and nothing
else.

## Open questions

  be broken (e.g. a Town Center as the bootstrap dropsite)?
- Should banked starting wood (`HasResourceAmount`) count, or only easy income?
