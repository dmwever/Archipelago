# 17: Buildings "standing or buildable" are never paid for

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split from [#05](05-attila1-forging-without-town-center.md) during triage

**Status:** done

**Blocked by:** #05

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The #05 trace shows the age-up "Any 2 of" buildings (Dock, Mill) and the Blacksmith as "standing or
buildable" in Attila 1 with no wood income at all. Reproduced in a rule test on 2026-10-22: with
every item except Bleda's Camp and the two Town Center items, `has_building` is True for the Dock
(150 wood), Mill (100 wood), Blacksmith (150 wood), Lumber Camp and Mining Camp (100 wood each).

## Audit (from code)

`ScenarioLogic.has_building` is `starts_with_building[building] | buildings.can_build_building(building)`.
The buildable half is structural: civilisation, scenario exclusions, and `ScenarioCanBuild` (build
permission plus builders). It never asks `economy.can_pay(building.cost)`.

Callers that inherit the unpaid building include the age-up requirement, the tech-building check,
every dropsite question (`has_wood_dropsite`, `has_food_dropsite`, ...), `is_fortified`, and
`can_hold_a_shoreline`. The Build locations themselves do pay (`Logic.can_build_location_anywhere`
asks `can_pay(building.cost)`), so only the rules that lean on a building are unpaid.

After #05 lands, ages need a base, which needs the Town Center items, so a scenario with no wood
income still has 275 banked wood from the Town Center item. That covers the Town Center and nothing
else.

## Open questions

- Pay everywhere `has_building` is asked, or only where the building feeds an age-up or a research?
- **Cycle hazard:** `has_wood_dropsite` asks for a Lumber Camp; paying for it asks `can_pay(wood)`,
  i.e. easy wood, which asks `has_wood_dropsite`. Does rule_builder resolve that, or does it need to
  be broken (e.g. a Town Center as the bootstrap dropsite)?
- Should banked starting wood (`HasResourceAmount`) count, or only easy income?
- Fill impact across every scenario: needs the replay gate used in #05.

### Triage Notes (2026-10-22)

**Reproduced (rule test, not in game):** Attila 1, every pooled item except Bleda's Camp and both
Town Center items, default options: easy wood is False, yet `ScenarioLogic.has_building` is True for
the Dock, Mill, Blacksmith, Lumber Camp, Mining Camp and Town Center. None is paid.

**Cycle hazard confirmed:** patching `_has_building` to add `economy.can_pay(building.cost)` fails
at world build with
`RecursionError: ('ScenarioHasEasyResource', <Age2ScenarioData.AP_ATTILA_1: 101>, <Resource.WOOD: 1>) asks itself; the rule would never terminate`
(the guard in `ScenarioQuestion.answered`). Paying inside `has_building` itself is not possible.

**ADR-0002 conflict:** its Consequences said `has_building` never pays. Resolved below by giving
buildings the paid/structural pair the other locations already have; the ADR is amended.

**Grilling (2026-10-22):**

- **Buildings get the pair** techs, ages and units already have: `can_build_structurally` /
  `can_build` (paid), and `has_building_structurally` / `has_building` (paid). Standing costs nothing.
- **A paid form pays for every building it leans on; a structural form pays for nothing.** That
  covers the paid age climb (age-up buildings), a tech's research building, a unit's training
  building, a Build location's prerequisite, and Attila 4's alliance.
- **The economy stays structural** (dropsites, `is_fortified`, `can_hold_a_shoreline`, market,
  dock, Monastery). That keeps the cycle closed. After #05 every gathered resource asks for a base,
  whose Town Center drops off everything, so no economy route runs on an unpayable building.
- **The Town Center is paid by the base, never by an easy source.** A standing one (Bleda's Camp,
  Attila 3's) is free; a built one is paid by the Town Center items, reached through the location's
  own food cost. Paying it with `can_pay` would ask easy stone of every age.
- **No bank.** A building's price is easy sources only, no `HasResourceAmount`: the bank counts the
  Town Center items (double spend) and cannot sum two buildings. Banked seeds stay for one-off
  purchases (fishing ship, trade seed).
- **Attila 4's "can ally with Burgundy"** is folded in: it asked the global
  `BuildingLogic.has_building` (items only, no scenario, unpaid) for the Market and Castle, and asked
  `has_base()` directly. It moves to the scenario's paid `has_building` and drops `has_base()`.
- **The ~40 direct `has_base()` calls in defeat rules** go to [#18](18-military-base-through-price.md).
- **Renames:** `can_build_location_anywhere` -> `can_build_anywhere`, `can_build_building_anywhere`
  -> `can_build_structurally_anywhere`, `BuildingLogic.has_building` -> `has_building_items`.
- **Fill gate:** same as #05, measured after #05 lands.
- ADR: [0002](../../../worlds/age2de/docs/adr/0002-base-reached-only-through-price.md) Consequences
  amended.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-22 after grilling.

**Category:** bug
**Blocked by:** #05. Start only once #05 is done; its climb and base changes are assumed here.
**Summary:** A location pays for the buildings it leans on, through a paid/structural pair for buildings

**Current behavior:**
`ScenarioLogic.has_building` is "standing, or buildable" and never pays. Every location that leans
on a building inherits it unpaid: the age climb's "Any 2 of" age-up buildings, a tech's research
building (`ScenarioTechLogic.available`), a unit's training building (`ScenarioCanTrain`), and a
Build location's prerequisite (`ScenarioCanBuild`). In Attila 1 with no wood income, Feudal Age and
Research Forging are in logic though no Mill, Dock or Blacksmith could be paid for. Only the Build
location itself pays, inline in `Logic.can_build_location_anywhere`. Attila 4's "can ally with
Burgundy" asks the global `BuildingLogic.has_building` (the items alone) for its Market and Castle,
and `has_base()` directly.

**Desired behavior:**
- Buildings get the same paid/structural pair as techs, ages and units:
  - `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_build_structurally(building)`: today's `can_build_building`, renamed.
  - `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_build(building)`: `can_build_structurally(building)` and
    `economy.can_pay(building.cost)`, except the Town Center (below).
  - `ScenarioLogic.has_building_structurally(building)`: standing, or `can_build_structurally`.
    Today's `has_building`, renamed.
  - `ScenarioLogic.has_building(building)`: standing, or `can_build`. The paid form.
- **Town Center:** its paid form is its structural form. A standing one is free; a built one is paid
  by the base (the Town Center items), which the location reaches through its own price. Never
  `can_pay(TOWN_CENTER.cost)`.
- **A paid form pays for every building it leans on; a structural form pays for nothing:**
  - Paid age climb (`ScenarioAgeLogic.can_reach`): pays for its age-up buildings, by re-asking the
    age-up requirement with the paid `has_building` (still "two of", or a Castle into Imperial). The
    structural climb (`climb`, `can_reach_structurally`) asks `has_building_structurally`.
  - Paid tech (`ScenarioTechLogic.can_research`): additionally asks the paid `has_building` for any
    of the tech's research buildings. `can_research_structurally` and `ScenarioCanResearch` stay
    structural.
  - Paid fielding (`ScenarioUnitLogic._field(paid=True)`): each trained tier additionally asks the
    paid `has_building` for any of its training buildings. `can_train_structurally`,
    `ScenarioCanTrain` and `_field(paid=False)` stay structural.
  - Build location: `can_build` on a building with a prerequisite pays for the prerequisite too
    (the paid `has_building` of the prerequisite).
- **The economy stays structural:** every dropsite question, `is_fortified` / `_fortified`,
  `can_hold_a_shoreline`, market and dock questions, the Monastery, `_at_scale`, `_chop_wood`, and
  all of `ScenarioHasEasyResource` call the structural forms. No easy source may ask a paid building.
- **No bank:** a building's price never accepts `HasResourceAmount`.
- **Attila 4** (`rules/attila_rules/attila_4.py`), "can ally with Burgundy": the scenario's paid
  `has_building` for MARKET and CASTLE, `ages.can_reach(CASTLE)` as today, and no `has_base()`.
- **Renames:**
  - `Logic.can_build_location_anywhere` -> `can_build_anywhere` (paid: `is_unlocked` and
    `can_build`, no inline price).
  - `Logic.can_build_building_anywhere` -> `can_build_structurally_anywhere` (tech and unit doors,
    LocalStart: behaviour unchanged).
  - `BuildingLogic.has_building` -> `has_building_items` (its prerequisites and Joan 3's base
    requirement: behaviour unchanged).
- /explain names: the paid `has_building` reads "has a Mill, standing or paid for"; the structural
  one keeps "standing or buildable".
- Respect ADR-0002 as amended: add no direct `has_base()` anywhere.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic`: `can_build_structurally`, `can_build`.
- `ScenarioLogic`: `has_building_structurally`, `has_building` (paid).
- `ScenarioAgeLogic.can_reach`, `age_up_requirement` (takes which `has_building` to use).
