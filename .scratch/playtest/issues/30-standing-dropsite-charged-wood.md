# 30: A standing Mill, Mining Camp or Folwark is still charged *some* wood

**Reported:** 2026-10-26, found while triaging [#28](28-joan4-french-camp-base-unrecorded.md)

**Status:** needs-triage

**Related:** [#24](24-building-seed-items.md) (introduced the funding), ADR-0002

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

ADR-0002, as [#24](24-building-seed-items.md) amended it, says of the buildings an economy route
stands on: *"A standing one is free."* The **seeded** buildings honour that -
`ScenarioLogic._has_seeded_building` reads `starts_with_building[building]` first, so a standing
Lumber Camp, Mule Cart, Dock or Market needs no seed item.

The **non-seeded** ones do not. `ScenarioResourceLogic.villager_routes_of_some` attaches
`needs={Resource.WOOD: Strength.SOME}` to every Mill, Mining Camp and Folwark route
unconditionally, and `_gathers_some_at` never asks whether the building is standing:

```python
mill = self.scenario.has_building_structurally(Age2BuildingData.MILL)
...
EconomyRoute(
    name="Hunters at a Mill",
    yields={Resource.FOOD: Strength.SOME},
    leaf=self._gathers_some_at(mill, Job.HUNTER_MALE, "hunt_count"),
    needs=wood_of_some,          # charged whether or not the Mill is already there
),
```

So a scenario handed a Mill at the start is asked for *some* wood it never spends. The wood is
there to fund building one; there is nothing to fund.

This is a **tightening**, so nothing becomes unwinnable - it asks for more than reality does. It is
still wrong, and it is the direction that costs fill attempts.

## Open questions

- Is the fix "a standing building's route carries no `needs`", or "a non-seeded building gets the
  same `_has_seeded_building` treatment with *some* wood standing in for the item"? The second is
  closer to how the seeded ones read, and keeps the funding in one place rather than on the route.
- `needs` is per route, not per leaf, and a route's `needs` is a flat mapping. If the funding has
  to become conditional on a rule, it may not fit `EconomyRoute.needs` at all and may have to move
  into the leaf - which changes what the fixpoint can see. That is the real design question, and
  it is why this was not folded into #28.
- Does the route catalog change size? Splitting a route into standing and buildable forms would
  grow it; moving the funding into the leaf would not.
- Does the Monastery's relic route have the same problem? `relic_routes` carries
  `needs={Resource.WOOD: Strength.SOME}` and the Monastery is non-seeded, so probably yes.
