# 30: A standing Mill, Mining Camp or Folwark is still charged *some* wood

**Reported:** 2026-10-26, found while triaging [#28](28-joan4-french-camp-base-unrecorded.md)

**Status:** done

**Related:** [#24](24-building-seed-items.md) (introduced the funding), ADR-0002

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

ADR-0002, as [#24](24-building-seed-items.md) amended it, says of the buildings an economy route
stands on: *"A standing one is free."* The **seeded** buildings honour that -
`ScenarioLogic._has_seeded_building` reads `starts_with_building[building]` first, so a standing
Lumber Camp, Mule Cart, Dock or Market needs no seed item.

The **non-seeded** ones do not. `ScenarioResourceLogic.villager_routes_of_some` attaches
`needs={Resource.WOOD: Strength.SOME}` to every Mill, Mining Camp and Folwark route
unconditionally, and `_gathers_some_at` never asks whether the building is standing:

```python
mill = self.scenario.has_building_structurally(Age2BuildingData.MILL)
...
EconomyRoute(
    name="Hunters at a Mill",
    yields={Resource.FOOD: Strength.SOME},
    leaf=self._gathers_some_at(mill, Job.HUNTER_MALE, "hunt_count"),
    needs=wood_of_some,          # charged whether or not the Mill is already there
),
```

So a scenario handed a Mill at the start is asked for *some* wood it never spends. The wood is
there to fund building one; there is nothing to fund.

This is a **tightening**, so nothing becomes unwinnable - it asks for more than reality does. It is
still wrong, and it is the direction that costs fill attempts.

## Open questions

*Answered during triage - see Triage Notes below.*

- Is the fix "a standing building's route carries no `needs`", or "a non-seeded building gets the
  same `_has_seeded_building` treatment with *some* wood standing in for the item"? The second is
  closer to how the seeded ones read, and keeps the funding in one place rather than on the route.
- `needs` is per route, not per leaf, and a route's `needs` is a flat mapping. If the funding has
  to become conditional on a rule, it may not fit `EconomyRoute.needs` at all and may have to move
  into the leaf - which changes what the fixpoint can see. That is the real design question, and
  it is why this was not folded into #28.
- Does the route catalog change size? Splitting a route into standing and buildable forms would
  grow it; moving the funding into the leaf would not.
- Does the Monastery's relic route have the same problem? `relic_routes` carries
  `needs={Resource.WOOD: Strength.SOME}` and the Monastery is non-seeded, so probably yes.

## Comments

### Triage Notes (2026-10-26)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category: bug, not enhancement.** [#24](24-building-seed-items.md)'s approved spec already
decided this: *"A standing building is free. Funding takes the shape the base already uses: a
building that starts standing costs nothing, and only a built one asks for its seed."* It was
implemented for the four seeded buildings and not for the four funded by *some* wood. The code
diverges from a decision already taken, and ADR-0002 repeats that decision verbatim.

**All four open questions resolve without a grilling round:**

1. **Which shape.** Split the route. #24 set the precedent for exactly this: *"A dropsite is a
   disjunction and the Town Center branch needs no purchase, so a route charged for a camp must
   not charge a player whose Town Center is doing the work. Each villager route of some therefore
   becomes several explicit routes."* The standing branch is that same disjunction one level
   deeper - `has_building_structurally` is literally
   `starts_with_building | can_build_structurally`.
2. **Can the funding move into the leaf?** It must not, and it need not. A leaf asking the economy
   would be the economy asking itself, which is why #24 used `needs` in the first place. And a
   need can never be conditional on state: `are_needs_met` reads a flat `Mapping` both in
   `running(state)` and in the state-free `possible` / `certain` fixpoints. Splitting is the only
   expressible shape, and `EconomyRoute` is unchanged - as #24 promised.
3. **Catalog size.** It grows, 53 -> 66 routes, one table. A conditional split - emitting the
   standing route only where `starts_with_building[b]` is not `False_()` - was built and measured
   first: it kept 13 scenarios at 53 but broke the catalog into four tables for a 277-line diff
   against 141, and it needed `isinstance(starts_with_building[b], False_)`, a build-time peek at
   rule identity. Rejected on the maintainer's call: always split, and keep the rest of the
   codebase clean.
4. **The Monastery.** Yes, the same defect, and fixed the same way - but it changes nothing
   observable today. Relics exist only in Attila 3, Attila 4 and Genghis 5; Genghis 4 is the sole
   standing Monastery and has `relic_count=0`. `Relics at a standing Monastery` reads 0/18 in the
   catalog. Correct by principle, dead in practice.

**Why `starts_with_building` cannot be resolved at build time.** It holds a `Rule`, and in four of
the five affected scenarios a non-constant one (`has_bledas_camp`, `can_reach_orleans`,
`kipchak_camp`, `camp`). Only Attila 3's Mill is `True_()`. So "is one standing?" is a question for
the state, not for route construction - which is what forces the split rather than a build-time
drop of the `needs`.

**Rejected approach, recorded.** Two new rules on `ScenarioLogic` - `has_building_standing` and
`can_put_up_building` - were written and then removed. `can_build_structurally` already exists, is
public, and is already called from outside `ScenarioLogic` (`explain/Routes.py`, `logic/Logic.py`);
wrapping it added only a `Named` layer over `ScenarioCanBuild`, which already describes itself. The
standing half is one dict read that `ScenarioResourceLogic` is already entitled to make - it reads
`starting_state.must_steal_base` two methods up. Using the two operands directly is also what makes
"together they still cover `has_building_structurally`" checkable by eye.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** A camp funded by *some* wood is charged only on the branch that puts one up; a
standing one is free, as ADR-0002 has said since #24.

**Current behavior**

Every Mill, Mining Camp, Folwark and Monastery route carries `needs={WOOD: SOME}` whatever the
scenario starts with. Five scenarios are handed one of these standing and are charged for it
anyway, across 30 routes.

**Desired behavior**

*A funded camp is two routes, not one.* The dropsite is a disjunction, so each funding state gets
its own route: the standing one carries no `needs`, the built one carries `{WOOD: SOME}`. Their
leaves are the two halves of `has_building_structurally`, so together they still cover exactly what
the single route covered.

*Nothing else moves.* `EconomyRoute` is unchanged, the Town Center branches stay free, the seeded
buildings keep handling their own standing case inside `has_seeded_building`, and a Dock splits
only where its seed is not pooled - where it is, it is already one route that is free when standing.

*The split is unconditional.* Every scenario builds both routes, whether or not it can have one
standing, so no rule-identity inspection decides route shape and the catalog stays a single table.

**Key interfaces**

- `ScenarioResourceLogic._at_a_funded_camp(who, camp, job, field, resource)` returns the two routes
  for one villager route of *some*. Call sites splice it with `*`, one per route, so a route is
  still edited by editing its own declaration (#24's hand-written rule).
- `ScenarioResourceLogic._at_a_dock(who, job, field, resource)` keeps the seeded Dock as one route
  and defers to `_at_a_funded_camp` where the seed is not pooled.
- `_collect_relics(monastery=None)` takes the Monastery rule so the two relic routes can name one
  half each; left unsaid it is the whole structural Monastery, which is what the existing callers
  and `test_scan_decides_resources` ask for.
- No new rule on `ScenarioLogic`. The two halves are
  `starting_state.starts_with_building[camp]` and `buildings.can_build_structurally(camp)`.

### Done (2026-10-26)

Committed by the maintainer as `11c1dd008` *"Given buildings make resources in logic at some"*,
touching `ScenarioResourceLogic.py` (+88 / -94) and the pinned `route_catalog.md`.

- **`_at_a_funded_camp`** builds the standing route (leaf
  `starts_with_building[camp]`, no needs) and the built route (leaf
  `buildings.can_build_structurally(camp)`, `needs={WOOD: SOME}`). Fifteen route declarations became
  fifteen spliced calls; `wood_of_some`, `dock` and `dock_needs` are gone from
  `villager_routes_of_some`, and `mill` / `folwark` / `mining_camp` now hold the building data
  rather than a pre-built rule.
- **`_at_a_dock`** keeps the seeded Dock as one route and falls back to `_at_a_funded_camp` when
  the seed is not pooled, preserving #24's "a Dock with partner trade off is funded like a Mill".
- **`relic_routes`** became *Relics at a standing Monastery* (free) and *Relics at a built
  Monastery* (charged), through a new optional `monastery` parameter on `_collect_relics`.
- **Route names** are now "<who> at a standing <camp>" / "<who> at a built <camp>". The bare
  "<who> at a <camp>" name is gone for funded camps; the Town Center, Lumber Camp, Mule Cart and
  Dock routes keep theirs.
- **Still uncommitted** for the maintainer: `test/test_seed_items.py`
  (`TestAStandingFundedCampIsFree`), the ADR-0002 amendment, this ticket, and the bench `.jsonl`
  once the run lands. The bench README's step 3 asks for that `.jsonl` to be committed.
