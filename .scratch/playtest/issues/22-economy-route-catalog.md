**Summary:** Declare each economy route's yields and economy seed per the settled catalog, so the base stops being the only way to an economy

**Current behavior:**
After #19, the routes reproduce the old rules exactly. Trade Carts and Fishing Ships take wood only
from villagers chopping with a base (or, for Fishing Ships, 75 banked wood), never from cogs. Trade
Cogs take wood only from villagers chopping with a base or 400 banked. Farming asks no base. Market
trade is seeded only by easy wood or endless food. Gold from Fishing Ships (oysters by boat, whales)
asks a base and a mining camp.

**Desired behavior:** the catalog below. "Some X" means a route need of X at strength *some* (any
running route yielding X, at either strength); "X at scale" means a route need at *at scale*. Tier
gates and the map's scanned amount against the scenario's demand apply as today wherever a route
works resources on the map.

| Route | Yields | Strength | Economy seed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villagers gathering (each job) | that job's resource | some | villagers (any number), the job, its dropsite, the resource on a tier whose gate holds (see *Per source*) |
| Villagers gathering, with a base | same | at scale | the above + **base** + each source's camp (see *Per source*), weighed against demand |
| Farming (farm, pasture) | food | at scale | **base**, the building, the job, any food dropsite (no camp), **wood at scale** |
| Fishing Ships (deep fish, shore fish, oysters by boat, whales) | food, gold | at scale | Fishing Ship item, a fishing-boat dropsite (Dock or Harbor: the whole camp), **wood at scale**, the resource on the map. **No age**, **no base** and **no bank** |
| Villagers and Fishing Ships | food, gold | at scale | both rows above summed against demand: each villager term with the base and its camp, each boat term with the boat seed; **wood at scale** |
| Fish traps | food | at scale | Fishing Ship item, a fishing-boat dropsite, Fish Trap, **wood at scale**. No map count, no tier, no guard |
| Trade Cogs | wood, gold | at scale | a trade partner's dock, Feudal, a Dock, Trade Cog item, **some wood** or 400 banked, **some gold** or 100 banked |

A route of *some* weighs no demand and asks no camp, but does ask the tier gates. A shore job of
*some* sits under the shoreline gate, so it gets guard variants like any other route behind it.

- A bank is *some* only for an economy seed whose authored amount it covers (`TRADE_SEED_GOLD`,
  `TRADE_SEED_WOOD_AT_SEA`). The Fishing Ship's 75-wood bank check goes.
- The naval guard (folded in from [#21](21-structural-field-asks-price.md)) is not a route: it is
  part of the economy seed of every route working through the afloat or shoreline gates. #19 leaves
  its navy cost as route needs **at scale**; lower them to **some**. No bank pays for the guard: its
  wood and gold come only from a running route (a few villagers chopping, relics, a little mining,
  cogs, a scenario's *some* hook), never from `HasResourceAmount`.
- With no base and no trade partner's dock, no route reaches *at scale*: that is intended. The cog is
  the only route an economy seed of *some* can start.
- `has_base()` stays the base exactly as #05 leaves it.

### Grilling (2026-10-23)

Raised while implementing [#21](21-structural-field-asks-price.md), whose contract is this ticket.
The brief's catalog left each source's camp, tiering and sums open; settled one source at a time:

- **Camp = the job's dropsites less the Town Center, or more Town Centers.** The base's TC is
  always a dropsite, so "a dropsite" at scale says nothing; the camp is the second building. Mule
  Cart counts for wood, gold, stone and hunt (no shipped civ builds one, so no shipped answer moves).
- **Per source:** hunt and forage ask a Mill or Folwark (hunt also a Mule Cart); herding asks no camp,
  as sheep walk to the base; shore fish and shore oysters ask a Mill, Folwark or Dock (the oyster job
  already drops at fisherman dropsites); Farms and Pastures ask none, as they sit by an existing
  dropsite. Fishing Ships' Dock or Harbor in the seed is their whole camp: one is enough.
- **The camp gates each term of the demand sum**, not the sum: a Mining Camp does nothing for
  oyster gatherers.
- **Villagers and Fishing Ships keep one sum** through a third route summing both, with wood at
  scale; each alone stays a route. Boats work shore fish too, under the shoreline gate.
- **Routes of *some* ask the tier gates** (not as today: `can_mine_some` and the like read the map
  total). No demand, no camp. Shore jobs of *some* sit under the shoreline gate, guard included.
- **Herd ignores the open-ground gate** at both strengths (the herder does not stay out there);
  enemy ground keeps its gate and ally ground never counts.
- **Relics** drop the villagers and gold dropsite `can_mine_some` brought, and are tier-gated.
- **Fish Traps** ask no map count, tier or guard.
- **Criterion 1** cannot hold as written (nothing yields the cogs' gold): two subtests, a gold bank
  and *some* mining.
- **The naval guard criterion** cannot hold as written (Fishing Ships need wood at scale): split
  into deep fish (gold of *some*) and shoreline villagers (wood and gold of *some*; "a few
  lumberjacks is enough to get a very small defensive navy started").
- **The equivalence snapshot** is recaptured after this ticket, to guard the next catalog change.
- Spun off: [#24](24-building-seed-items.md), items seeding the economy's buildings and units.

## Implementation (2026-10-23)

> *This was generated by AI during implementation.*

- `ScenarioResourceLogic.own_routes()` is the catalog: villager routes of *some* per job (tiered,
  shore jobs with guard variants), villagers with a base at scale (each term with its camp), Farmers,
  Fishing Ships for food and for gold, Villagers and Fishing Ships summed (a shore counted once,
  worked by either), Fish Traps, Trade Cogs and Trade Carts (bank variants via `_banked_routes`),
  Relics. Market Trade is one route per resource sold.
- Guard variants add the navy's costs as needs of *some* (`setdefault`, so a stronger need stays).
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic.has_*_camp()`: the job's dropsites less the TC, or more TCs. Camps now read
  `has_building_structurally` (standing or buildable), where #19 read `can_build_structurally`.
- Deleted: `can_crew_fishing_ships`, `can_fish_some`, `can_gather_gold`, `can_gather_wood`,
  `has_infinite_food`/`_fish`, `endless_*`, `_can_seed_trade_gold`, `_can_get_wood_easily`.
  `has_fishing_ships` drops the age (fish trap building still asks its own age).
- Note: the summed villager-and-boat route asks the base of the whole sum, not only villager terms;
  without a base it reduces to the boat-only route, which exists, so no answer differs.
- Tests: `test/test_economy_catalog.py`, a toy scenario (Attila 6 with every leaf fact a switch).
