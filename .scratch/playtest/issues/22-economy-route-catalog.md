## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-22 after grilling.

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Declare each economy route's yields and economy seed per the settled catalog, so the base stops being the only way to an economy

**Current behavior:**
After #19, the routes reproduce the old rules exactly. Trade Carts and Fishing Ships take wood only
from villagers chopping with a base (or, for Fishing Ships, 75 banked wood), never from cogs. Trade
Cogs take wood only from villagers chopping with a base or 400 banked. Farming asks no base. Market
trade is seeded only by easy wood or endless food. Gold from Fishing Ships (oysters by boat, whales)
asks a base and a mining camp.

**Desired behavior:** the catalog below. "Some X" means a route need of X at strength *some* (any
running route yielding X, at either strength); "X at scale" means a route need at *at scale*. Tier
gates and the map's scanned amount against the scenario's demand apply as today wherever a route
works resources on the map.

| Route | Yields | Strength | Economy seed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villagers gathering (each job) | that job's resource | some | villagers (any number), the job, its dropsite, the resource on the map |
| Villagers gathering, with a base | same | at scale | the above + **base** + a camp or more Town Centers |
| Farming (farm, pasture) | food | at scale | **base**, the building, the job, a food dropsite, **wood at scale** |
| Fishing Ships (deep fish, oysters by boat, whales) | food, gold | at scale | Fishing Ship item, a fishing-boat dropsite, **wood at scale**, the resource on the map. **No age** and **no bank** |
| Fish traps | food | at scale | the Fishing Ships route's seed + Fish Trap + **wood at scale** |
| Trade Cogs | wood, gold | at scale | a trade partner's dock, Feudal, a Dock, Trade Cog item, **some wood** or 400 banked, **some gold** or 100 banked |
| Trade Carts | gold | at scale | a trade partner's market, Feudal, a Market, Trade Cart item, **wood at scale**, **some gold** or 100 banked |
| Market trade | every resource | at scale | a Market + **any resource at scale** |
| Relics | gold | some | as today (Monastery, Monk, its age, a gold dropsite, relics on the map) |
| Scenario routes | per scenario | at scale / some | `easy_resource_sources` / the *some* hook from #19 |

- A bank is *some* only for an economy seed whose authored amount it covers (`TRADE_SEED_GOLD`,
  `TRADE_SEED_WOOD_AT_SEA`). The Fishing Ship's 75-wood bank check goes.
- With no base and no trade partner's dock, no route reaches *at scale*: that is intended. The cog is
  the only route an economy seed of *some* can start.
- `has_base()` stays the base exactly as #05 leaves it.

**Key interfaces:**
- The route declarations from #19: route needs replace the leaf-part references that #19 kept for
  equivalence (`can_gather_wood()` inside carts, Fishing Ships, cogs; `_can_get_wood_easily() |
  endless_food()` inside market trade).
- `ScenarioResourceLogic`'s public questions (`can_crew_fishing_ships`, `can_run_trade_cogs`,
  `can_run_trade_carts`, `has_infinite_food`, …): keep them if anything outside the economy asks
  them, with the new meaning; delete the ones only the old trees used.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Maintainer's chain: a test scenario with three villagers, Lumberjack, a Lumber Camp, Trade
      Cogs, a trade partner's dock, Fishing Ships and a Dock, but **no base**: easy wood, easy gold
      and easy food all hold. Remove the Lumberjack (and any wood bank): none hold.
- [ ] No base, no trade partner's dock, everything else above plus Trade Carts and a partner's
      market: no easy source holds.
- [ ] Trade Carts run on cog wood with no base.
- [ ] 75 banked wood alone no longer crews Fishing Ships; Fishing Ships need wood at scale.
- [ ] Farming without a base is not an easy source of food; with a base and wood at scale it is.
- [ ] Market trade is seeded by easy gold alone (no easy wood, no endless food).
- [ ] Fishing Ship gold (oysters by boat, whales) is easy gold with no base and no mining camp, when
      wood at scale and the map's amount allow.
- [ ] 50 banked wood does not seed cogs; 400 does.
- [ ] Relics alone never make easy gold.
- [ ] `AGEIPELAGO_PATH=<Ageipelago> python -m pytest worlds/age2de/test` passes. If an existing test
      asserted old behaviour, change it to the new behaviour and list each such test in this issue.
- [ ] **Fill gate.** Replay the 63 generations in
      `worlds/age2de/fuzz/fuzz_output_lock_items/error/age2de/` before and after; the failure count
      must not rise. If it rises, stop: record which generations newly fail and the fill error, and
      hand back. Do not loosen any rule to make the fill pass. Record both counts here.
