## Report

## Tooling

**AoE2ScenarioParser does all three fields.** `Trigger` exposes `description`,
`short_description` and `display_as_objective` as read/write attributes linked to the binary
fields, and ships a v1.59 definition, which is what these scenarios are. Proved end to end on a
copy of `AP_Genghis_2`: set the flag and both strings on the three check triggers, wrote the
file, verified with `kit` rather than ASP's own readback. Trigger count held at 30 and the
`AP Give Tayichiud Base` grant trigger stayed hidden.

**aoe2kit cannot.** `EditTrigger` (`pkg/scenario/writer.go`) writes `enabled`, `looping`,
`trigger_name` and the two string-table *ids* only. `display_as_objective`, `trigger_description`
and `short_description` are on the recipe struct and written by `buildTriggerRaw` for
`add_trigger`, but `edit_trigger` never applies them. Use `kit` to read and to diff, not to
write this.

Three things to expect from an ASP pass:

- **Round-trip drift.** `kit scen diff` on the proof run showed 13 changes, 4 intended. The
  others: `color_mood` `"Winter"` -> `"winter"`, and `ai_type` `0` -> `1` on P10-P16, the
  inactive slots past the eight real players. Diff every scenario and judge the drift before
  keeping the write.
- **ASP's XS linter is noisy here.** It lints embedded `script_call` text on write and raises
  `NameError: MakeKushlukFlee is not defined`, because those functions live in the mod's XS
  files. Expected noise, not a failure.
- **Console encoding.** ASP prints emoji and dies with `UnicodeEncodeError` on cp1252 before
  doing any work. `sys.stdout.reconfigure(encoding="utf-8")` in the script; `PYTHONIOENCODING`
  is ignored under `python -I`.

## Display order

Objectives sort by `objective_description_order`, and a header trigger carries the group whose
orders sit *below* its own. Joan and Attila follow one rule, checked across all twelve scenarios:
the `-- AP --` header takes one less than the lowest order already used by that scenario's
objectives, and the AP checks count down from there. It holds in eleven; Attila 6 is the lone
exception and is unfinished in other ways too (three of its displayed triggers have no short
description), so it is not a second convention. `AP Victory` takes the lowest order of the block
everywhere except Joan 2.

**The `-- AP --` trigger already exists in all six Genghis scenarios** with
`display_as_objective = 0`, `make_header = 0` and order `0`. It needs enabling, not creating.

| scenario | lowest existing order | `-- AP --` header | checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AP_Genghis_1 | 50 | 49 | 48 down to 26 |
| AP_Genghis_2 | 48 | 47 | 46 down to 44 |
| AP_Genghis_3 | 49 | 48 | 47 down to 40 |
| AP_Genghis_4 | 48 | 47 | 46 down to 42 |
| AP_Genghis_5 | 24 | 23 | 22 down to 15 |
| AP_Genghis_6 | 48 | 47 | 46 down to 45 |

Two anchors needed a judgement call, recorded on each header row in the table. Genghis 1 leaves
seven `[Obj]` triggers at order `0` and Genghis 4 one, so the anchor is the lowest *non-zero*
order; Joan 1 has the same shape and was authored the same way. Genghis 5 anchors on its
Secondary Objective block at 24/25, not the Main Objective block at 47-50, because 24 is lower.
