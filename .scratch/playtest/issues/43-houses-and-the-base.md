## Report

#42 took the base out of the age climb: a climb now asks a Town Center standing or paid with the
Town Center items, and none of the base's Villager Line, villager food, ground or House. That left
two questions for the maintainer.

**1. Houses.** In logic, the House is asked only for the base:

- `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_build_base` (`logic/scenarios/ScenarioBuildingLogic.py:68-71`): the
  base's Town Center plus a House, structurally, where the civilisation builds Houses. It reaches
  the rules through `can_have_base` → `has_base`.
- `generation/LocalStart.py:136`: the local start target asks that a House can be built somewhere.
- Its own location, "Build House" (Base category).

Nothing pays for the House. #37's allowlist has 9 lines for it, all in economy routes, for example
`A Life of Revenge runs Lumberjacks with a Base: A Life of Revenge can build a House (unpaid: wood)`.
#39's item 6 (from #32) plans `wood: some` for Houses on every route that asks for a base, as the
payment for villagers at scale.

**2. The base itself.** The maintainer asked whether the base should go entirely, except for local
start. `has_base()` is asked today by:

- Local start (`generation/LocalStart.py:116`, `scenario_base_rule`).
- Farming (`ScenarioResourceLogic._farm`, `:234`).
- The at-scale villager routes: "Lumberjacks with a Base" (`:503`), villagers gathering food, gold and
  stone (`:516`, `:528`, `:540`), and the villager-and-boat food and gold routes (`:577`, `:590`).

ADR-0003 already makes the base one route's economy seed, not the economy. #32 added that the base
buys only the first villagers; more villagers and their Houses are *some* food and *some* wood on
every route that stands on the base. With those needs in place, what the base adds over "a Town
Center standing or paid, and villagers" is the Villager Line item, the Starting Villager Food item,
the scenario's ground (`meets_additional_base_requirements`, such as Joan 3's crossing) and the
House.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* The glossary's **Housing** and ADR-0003's housing
> consequence are the contract; the Comments below hold the rulings and the scenario audit.

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Make **housing** an economy-route resource: produced by a Houses route, a civilisation
that builds no Houses, and scenario grants; needed by every trained unit.

**Current behavior**

The House is asked only inside the base, and only where the base's Town Center has to be built:
`can_build_base` asks it structurally, and a scenario with a standing Town Center never asks for
the House at all, not even its item under Buildsanity. Nothing pays for a House. Nothing asks for
room for any unit P1 trains: villagers at scale, Fishing Ships, Trade Carts and Cogs, Monks, or a
fielded army.

**Desired behavior**

- **Housing is a route resource with the usual two strengths,** *some* and at scale. It is never an
  item `Resource`: no seed item, no bank and no Market Trade yields it, and the item pool never
  counts it.
- **Producers**, each an economy route:
  - **Houses**: yields housing at scale. Its leaf asks the House as buildable
    (`can_build_structurally`: the House item, builders, a civilisation that builds Houses, not
    excluded by the scenario), and it needs `wood: some`. A standing House excuses nothing. Its
    builders are the villagers `has_builders` already asks, so it needs no housing itself.
  - **Builds no Houses**: yields housing at scale for nothing, for a civilisation that cannot build
    the House (the Huns).
  - **Scenario grant**: a starting-state rule granting housing at a strength. Default none. The
    grants are in the table in the Comments.
  - All three read no gate, so they are ungated routes, and Farmers and "Lumberjacks with a Base"
    stay ungated.
- **Consumers.** A producer that already stands needs none; a trained one needs housing:
  - Every route whose producer is trained at scale needs `housing: at scale`: the villager routes
    at scale, Farmers, the Fishing Ship routes, Fish Traps, Trade Carts and Trade Cogs.
  - Relics need `housing: some` only where no Monk stands.
  - Villager routes of *some* on standing or granted villagers (`has_vils`) need none.
  - Every force P1 fields needs housing at scale beside its price: `has_military`, `has_navy`,
    `counters`, siege, and the guards and holders of `_can_field_some`.
  - Every location that trains a single unit needs `housing: some`.
  - A fixed-force scenario trains nothing and asks no housing.
- **The House leaves the base.** `can_build_base` no longer asks for the House. Local start keeps
  the House in its target, now as the Houses route's item.
- Delete the #37 allowlist lines for "can build a House (unpaid: wood)"; the Houses route pays them.

**Key interfaces**

- `EconomyRoute.yields` / `needs` (and the resolved form) accept housing beside the item
  `Resource`s, through a key type the item enum does not contain, so Market Trade's "every
  `Resource` at scale" and `ResourcePool`'s tallies stay as they are.
- `ScenarioStartingState` gains a housing grant per strength, defaulting to none.
- A scenario question for housing at a strength, read like `has_easy_source` / `has_some_source`,
  for the consumers outside the routes (forces and single-unit locations).

- [ ] Joan 4: a single-unit location needs `camp` or the Houses route. Genghis 2: one is reachable
      from the start with no House item.
- [ ] Joan 3 and Genghis 3: a single-unit location needs the Houses route.
- [ ] Relics need no housing where a Monk stands, and *some* where none does.
- [ ] Market Trade yields no housing, and no item grants it.
- [ ] `can_build_base` asks no House; local start's target still asks the House where the

- [ ] The ungated-routes test still pins that nothing in that layer reaches a gate, with the
