## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

Tracing Attila 1's villager-less win under `allies_and_enemies` (#26) with `/explain`: Trade Cogs,
Trade Carts and Fish Traps all ask "Has reached the Feudal Age", which is
`ScenarioAgeLogic.has_reached(FEUDAL)`, the structural form. It asks the Feudal Age item, a Town
Center, and two Dark Age buildings, and **no food**. The age-up costs 500 food in game.

```
The Scourge of God: Has reached the Feudal Age -> True
    Has Feudal Age -> True
    Has a Town Center, standing or buildable -> True
    Any 2 of -> True
        Has a Mining Camp, standing or buildable
        Has a Dock, standing or buildable
        Has a Mill, standing or buildable
        Has a Lumber Camp, standing or buildable
        Has a Barracks, standing or buildable
```

An audit of every structural call site then found more of the same: things the logic asks for that
nothing ever pays for.

## Rulings

> *Stated by the maintainer, 2026-10-26.*

- **Nothing may ever be truly unpaid.** An unpaid requirement makes seeds impossible. Paying another
  way (a seed item, a bank, a route's `needs`, *some* of a cost) counts as paid. Not paying at all
  does not.
- **Ages must be paid for**, wherever they are asked, prerequisites included.
- **Attila 3's Build Castle location is wrong** as it stands (no price at all).
- **Owning a unit needs its training building standing, or buildable with payment.**

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Pay for every age, building and unit the logic asks, wherever it asks: ages at one
**age price**, the economy's capital through route needs or seed items, and locations through the
paid forms. Then add a test that fails on any structural form asked bare.

**Current behavior**

The structural forms (`has_reached`, `can_reach_structurally`, `can_build_structurally`,
`has_building_structurally`, `can_research_structurally`, `can_train_structurally`,
`can_field_structurally`, `has_researched_structurally`, and the plain dropsite and camp checks) pay
for nothing. Several rules ask one with no payment beside it, so the logic can put a check behind
something the player can never afford. The Facts section above lists every site. Triage confirmed
each one at `71f8dc02` and found two more: owning a Builder, and owning a Repairer in a scenario
that starts with no building, ask "can build anything" with nothing paid. Mass-producing villagers
is never paid either: the base buys the Town Center and the first three villagers, and nothing pays
for more villagers or their Houses.

**Desired behavior**

*Age price* (glossary: **Age price**). Reaching an age costs the same wherever it is asked, as a
location or as a prerequisite. An age the scenario starts in or past (the existing
`age <= start_age` / `age_playable` test in `ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for`) is free. A fixed force
still pays for no age.

| Age | Price |
|---|---|
| Feudal | *some* food, or the Feudal Age Food seed item |
| Castle | food at scale and *some* gold |
| Imperial | food and gold at scale (as today) |

`can_pay_for(age)` changes to these prices, so Reach Feudal and Reach Castle loosen. The prices
nest (Castle's easy food covers Feudal's *some*), so the paid climb pays nothing extra for the ages
below.

*Inside the economy.* An age, a building or a unit that an economy route waits on is capital
(glossary: **Economy seed**). Capital is paid through the route's `needs`, through a bank in the
route's leaf, or through a seed item. It is never paid by `has_easy_source`, `has_some_source` or
`can_pay`, because the route would then ask itself.

1. **Feudal for Trade Carts, Trade Cogs and Fish Traps.** Each route that asks Feudal, in a
   scenario starting below Feudal, comes in two versions: one with `food: some` added to its needs,
   and one with 500 banked food in its leaf instead. The cog routes already have bank versions;
   combine them rather than multiplying them blindly, and write the versions out as the trade routes
   are written today.
2. **Feudal Age Food**: a new `SeedResources(Resource.FOOD, 500)` item, id 1036. It is pooled by the
   #24 rule: only when at least one enabled scenario starts below Feudal and has a route that asks
   Feudal (a reachable trade partner under `partner_trade`, or water for Fish Traps). One per world.
   On the XS side (Ageipelago), add a `FeudalAgeFood()` that grants 500 food, like
   `StartingVillagerFood()`, and its `case 1036` in the progression-item switch. That switch has no
   `default`, so a missing case drops the item silently.
3. **Castle for relics.** The relic routes add `food: at scale, gold: some` (the Castle price; the
   *some* gold also covers the Monk's 100) when the scenario starts below Castle. Relics then never
   open a scenario's first gold. That is correct: Castle costs gold.
4. **A second Town Center** has two rules, not one:
   - **As a defence** (the strongpoint branch of `is_fortified`, and `can_hold_a_shoreline`): *some*
     of the Town Center's cost and the Castle price, both read from the ungated routes. The
     Castle's food at scale is `EconomyRoutesYield` over the ungated routes at `AT_SCALE`. This is
     new; the ungated layer is asked only at *some* today. Name it `tc_as_gate` or similar.
   - **As a camp** (`_camp_among`): its own route version per at-scale route. Its needs are the
     Castle price plus *some* wood and *some* stone. It never reads the ungated routes.
   - Neither asks the Town Center items. They paid for the base's Town Center and do not pay twice.
5. **Unseeded camps.** The at-scale villager routes split by dropsite, the same way the routes of
   *some* do (`_at_a_funded_camp`): a standing camp is free, a built camp needs `wood: some`, a
   seeded camp uses its seed item, and the second-Town-Center camp is item 4.
6. **Villagers at scale.** Every route that asks for a base (the at-scale villager routes, Farmers,
   and the villager-and-boat routes) adds `food: some` for training villagers, and `wood: some` for
   Houses where the scenario's civilisation builds Houses.
7. **The base's lumberjacks move to the ungated layer.** Once item 4 gives the camp its own rule, no
   part of "Lumberjacks with a Base" reads a gate, so the whole route moves from `gated_routes` to
   `ungated_routes`. Ungated Farmers then get their wood at scale in any scenario with a base, which
   is what makes a second Town Center reachable as a defence.
8. **The Mill in `can_hold_a_shoreline`** pays `can_pay_some(MILL.cost)` beside it, as #23's
   defences do.

*Locations and other rules outside the economy*, where `can_pay` and `can_pay_some` are safe:

9. **Build locations** (`can_build`) also pay the building's age price.
10. **Tech locations** (`ScenarioTechLogic.can_research`) also pay the tech's age price.
11. **Unit locations** (`UnitLogic.can_train_anywhere`) also pay the unit's age price and a training
    building, standing or paid (`can_pay_for_training_building`), as paid fielding already does.
12. **Attila 3's Build Castle** becomes the paid build of a Castle (cost, prerequisite, Castle
    price). **Genghis 5's castles** add the Castle price to what they ask today.
13. **Owning a Builder or Repairer** asks, for "can build anything", a buildable building plus
    *some* of its cost: the shape of `ScenarioBuildingLogic._can_defend_with`.
14. **Fish Traps, relics, more Town Centers, and every other remaining `has_reached` call** pay the
    age price through one of the above. Nothing asks an age bare.

*Enforcement.* A test walks every location and entrance rule of two generated worlds: all
campaigns, techsanity `all`, unitsanity `all`, villager professions shuffled, unique units `both`,
every shuffled building, partner trade `allies_and_enemies`, market trade on, one world with
`existing_techs: vanilla` and one with `start_in_dark_age`. Every structural node
(`ScenarioCanBuild`, `ScenarioHasReached`, `ScenarioCanTrain`, `ScenarioCanResearch`, a "standing or
buildable" building, a plain dropsite) must have a payment beside it in its enclosing `And`:
`ScenarioHasEasyResource`, `ScenarioHasSomeResource`, `HasResourceAmount`, `EconomyRoutesYield`, or
a seed or Town Center item. Inside `EconomyRoutes`, a route's `needs` or a bank in its leaf counts
as a payment for its leaf. Exempt: nodes inside a structural question's own answer (its parts; the
question is paired where it is asked), region doors whose locations pay (the Tech and Unit doors),
and nodes that resolve always-true (an age started past). A prototype measured about 6 s per world
build and well under a second for the walk over ~10,000 nodes. Add the test to the logic canaries
in `.githooks/pre-commit`.

**Key interfaces**

- `ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for(age)`: returns the age price above, not `economy.can_pay(age.cost)`.
- `ScenarioAgeLogic.has_reached(age)`: stays the structural question. Each caller pairs it with
  the age price, or with route needs inside the economy.
- `EconomyRoute.needs`: carries the new capital (age, villagers, Houses, camps).
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic.can_build_multiple_tc`: splits into a defence rule and a camp rule.
- `ScenarioResourceLogic.ungated_routes` and `gated_routes`: "Lumberjacks with a Base" moves.
- `Age2ItemData`: a new `SeedResources` item, id 1036, "Feudal Age Food".

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Grilling session, 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-27.

Rulings worth keeping, because each one reversed a plausible-looking alternative:

- **The Feudal seed item came from the maintainer.** Paying Feudal with `has_some_source` inside a
  trade route loops, because trade is in the ungated layer that `has_some_source` reads. Paying it
  with the full easy price undoes #19. A bank of Starting Villager Food is too small.
- **The cog pattern was chosen over a graded discount.** The maintainer asked about a 50% discount
  per *some* food source. It was dropped for three reasons: `needs` answers yes or no and cannot
  count sources; a count would depend on how the routes happen to be split; and the ruling "*some*
  counts as paid" already makes one source enough.
- **One price per age.** Two prices for one age, inside and outside the economy, would read as
  arbitrary in `/explain`. Reach Feudal and Reach Castle loosen as a result.
- **Castle food is always easy.** A second Town Center as a defence therefore needs food at scale
  out of the ungated layer, and it is viable only where ungated Farmers run. That is what moved the
  base's lumberjacks into the ungated layer: they were gated only because their camp choice
  included the second Town Center's defence rule.
- **Villagers at scale were unpaid too.** Found while checking whether Farmers could run: the base
  buys three villagers, and "at scale" assumed the rest for free.
- **Prefer "defence" to "strongpoint"** for a second Town Center, to match the code.
- **The tree-walk test was chosen over private structural forms.** Python cannot make the forms
  private without ruff's `SLF001`, the economy needs them from another module, and a rename cannot
  be proven red. The maintainer wants more invariant tests of this kind in pre-commit, so that
  question moves to #36.
- **The equivalence snapshot is not recaptured here.** It changes on every deliberate logic change
  and cannot say whether a change is correct; #36 decides its future.
- Contradicts ADR-0002 ("Prerequisites stay structural") and the "has_reached stays structural"
  decisions of #05, #17 and #19. The ADR-0002 amendment records the reversal.
