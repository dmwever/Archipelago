# 32: Nothing may be unpaid: ages, buildings and units the logic asks without a price

**Status:** needs-triage

**Related:** [#05](05-attila1-forging-without-town-center.md), [#17](17-buildable-buildings-unpaid.md), [#19](19-trade-cogs-without-base.md), [#24](24-building-seed-items.md), [#26](26-dock-guard-rulings.md), [#30](30-standing-dropsite-charged-wood.md)

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

Tracing Attila 1's villager-less win under `allies_and_enemies` (#26) with `/explain`: Trade Cogs,
Trade Carts and Fish Traps all ask "Has reached the Feudal Age", which is
`ScenarioAgeLogic.has_reached(FEUDAL)`, the structural form. It asks the Feudal Age item, a Town
Center, and two Dark Age buildings, and **no food**. The age-up costs 500 food in game.

```
The Scourge of God: Has reached the Feudal Age -> True
    Has Feudal Age -> True
    Has a Town Center, standing or buildable -> True
    Any 2 of -> True
        Has a Mining Camp, standing or buildable
        Has a Dock, standing or buildable
        Has a Mill, standing or buildable
        Has a Lumber Camp, standing or buildable
        Has a Barracks, standing or buildable
```

An audit of every structural call site then found more of the same: things the logic asks for that
nothing ever pays for.

## Rulings

> *Stated by the maintainer, 2026-10-26.*

- **Nothing may ever be truly unpaid.** An unpaid requirement makes seeds impossible. Paying another
  way (a seed item, a bank, a route's `needs`, *some* of a cost) counts as paid. Not paying at all
  does not.
- **Ages must be paid for**, wherever they are asked, prerequisites included.
- **Attila 3's Build Castle location is wrong** as it stands (no price at all).
- **Owning a unit needs its training building standing, or buildable with payment.**

### Actually unpaid

**1. Ages asked as prerequisites** (`ScenarioAgeLogic.has_reached`, structural):

| Site | Asks | Pays the age elsewhere? |
|---|---|---|
| `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_run_trade_carts` (`:271`) | Feudal | no |
| `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_run_trade_cogs` (`:282`) | Feudal | no |
| Fish Traps (`ScenarioCanBuild`, the Fish Trap's own age) | Feudal | no |
| relics (`ScenarioResourceLogic._collect_relics`, `:221`) | the Monk's age (Castle) | no |
| `ScenarioBuildingLogic._multiple_tc` (`:77`) | Castle | no |
| `ScenarioCanBuild` (`ScenarioQuestions.py:157`) | the building's age | the Build location pays the building's cost, not its age |
| `ScenarioCanResearch` (`ScenarioQuestions.py:189`) | the tech's age | the tech location pays the tech's cost and building, not its age |
| `ScenarioCanTrain` (`ScenarioQuestions.py:217`) | the unit's age | paid fielding pays the unit's own age; the unit *location* does not |
| `ScenarioTechLogic.has_tech` (`:65`) | the tech's age | no |
| the age climb (`ScenarioAgeLogic.py:145`) | the age before | the paid climb (`can_reach`) pays only the age being reached |

The paid price is `economy.can_pay(age.cost)`, an easy source per resource. Feudal costs food only,
so Feudal is short wherever nothing else paid asks easy food.

**2. Build locations with no price.**
- Attila 3's Build Castle (`rules/attila_rules/attila_3.py:25`): `can_build_structurally(CASTLE)`
  alone, so no stone and no Castle Age. #17 noted it as "unchanged".
- Genghis 5's castles (`rules/genghis_rules/genghis_5.py:35`): easy stone and the German defeat,
  but the Castle Age is unpaid.

**3. Unit locations.** `UnitLogic.can_train_anywhere` pays the unit's cost only. The training building
is reached through a region door asking `can_build_structurally_anywhere` (`rules/UnitRules.py:68`),
and the unit's age is unpaid. Paid fielding (`_field(paid=True)`) pays both; the location does not.

**4. The Monk for relics.** `_collect_relics` asks `has_unit_items(MONK)` (or a granted Monk) and the
Castle Age through `has_reached`. The Monk's 100 gold and the Castle Age are never paid. The
Monastery is paid; see below.

**5. Camps behind the at-scale villager routes.** "Villagers gathering gold / stone / food with a
Base" source from `has_gold_camp` / `has_stone_camp` / `has_food_camp` / `has_hunt_camp`
(`ScenarioBuildingLogic._camp_among`). `_camp` is the seeded form only when the camp's seed item is
in the pool; otherwise it is "standing or buildable". A Mining Camp or Mill has no seed item, and
these routes carry no `needs` (`-` in `test/route_catalog.md`), so the camp's 100 wood is never
asked. Any seeded camp falls back the same way when its seed is not in the pool.

**6. Plain dropsite checks.** `has_gold_dropsite`, `has_stone_dropsite`, `has_wood_dropsite`,
`has_food_dropsite`, `has_hunt_dropsite`, `has_fishing_boat_dropsite` and `has_fisherman_dropsite`
(`ScenarioBuildingLogic._any_of`) are always "standing or buildable". They are asked by mining,
quarrying, shore fishing, deep fishing, whaling and farming. They are paid only where the asking
route carries a `needs`.

**7. The Mill in `can_hold_a_shoreline`** (`ScenarioBuildingLogic.py:97`):
`has_building_structurally(MILL) & is_fortified()`. The fortification pays *some*; the Mill pays
nothing.

### Paid another way (not in scope)

- **Market and Dock for trade and for Fishermen at a Dock:** `has_seeded_building`, the seed item
  ("Starting Market Wood" 175, "Starting Dock Wood" 150) plus the rest of the cost banked.
- **Seeded camps:** Lumber Camp and Mule Cart through their seed items, when in the pool.
- **Built camps in the routes of *some*** (Mining Camp, Mill, Folwark): the route `needs: wood some`.
- **A built Monastery for relics:** the route `needs: wood some`.
- **The Fish Traps route:** `needs: wood at scale` covers the Dock and traps as wood flowing, not an
  exact cost check. The Fishing Ship routes are the same.
- **`is_fortified`, #23's guards and defences:** `can_pay_some` of each building or line.
- **Villager location:** easy food for 150 (Starting Villager Food's amount), and a Town Center
  standing or buildable, where buildable means holding both Town Center items, the Town Center's
  payment. Dark Age, so no age to pay.
- **Joan 3's transport** (`logic/joan/joan_3.py:40`): structural, pays its own cost inline.

## Acceptance criteria (proposed)

> *Added at the maintainer's request, 2026-10-26.* Triage completes the list.

- [ ] **No structural form is ever asked bare.** `has_building_structurally`, `can_build_structurally`,
      `can_reach_structurally`, `has_reached`, `can_research_structurally`, `can_train_structurally`,
      `can_field_structurally`, `has_researched_structurally` and the plain dropsite checks
      (`_any_of`) may appear only paired with a payment: a paid form, a seed item, a bank, a route
      `needs`, or *some* of the cost. A new rule that asks one alone must fail the build or a test,
      not wait for a playtest. Whether this is done by making the structural forms private to the
      modules that pair them, or by a test that walks every location's rule tree, is a triage
      question (below).

## Open questions

- **The loop.** A paid age inside the economy loops: easy food and gold ask trade, trade asks Feudal,
  and paying Feudal asks easy food. Likewise a paid dropsite inside an easy source. What pays without
  looping: a bank (Starting Villager Food is 150), *some* (`has_some_source`, as #25's guards), a
  route `needs` (as the built camps), or the full easy price? The full price undoes #19's base-free
  cogs.
- **Ages in the climb:** does the paid climb pay every age below it, or only its own?
- **Unseeded camps:** give the Mining Camp and Mill seed items (as #24), or add `needs: wood some`
  to the at-scale routes?
- **The Monk:** pay its cost from `has_some_source(GOLD)`, or as the routes of *some* do?
- **Enforcing "never bare":** structural forms made private to the modules that pair them with a
  payment (an import or name error on misuse), or a test walking each location's resolved rule tree
  for a structural form with no paid sibling (catches misuse anywhere, but must recognise every way
  of paying)? The scenario questions (`ScenarioCanBuild` and siblings) and the Tech/Unit region doors
  ask structural forms by design and need a place in whichever is chosen.
- Fill gate and equivalence recapture, as for #22.
