**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Give every age its one price (#32's *Age price* table), and make every location and
non-economy rule that asks an age, a building or a unit pay for it: #32's items 9 to 14.

**Current behavior**

`ScenarioAgeLogic.can_pay_for(age)` is `economy.can_pay(age.cost)`. Build, Tech and Unit locations
pay a cost but not the age they stand on. A Unit location does not pay for a training building.
Attila 3's Build Castle asks the Castle structurally and nothing else, and Genghis 5's castles do
not pay the Castle Age. Owning a Builder, or a Repairer where no building stands at the start, asks
"can build anything" with nothing paid.

**Desired behavior**

All rules here are outside the economy, so `can_pay` and `can_pay_some` are safe.

- `can_pay_for(age)` returns the age price: Feudal is *some* food or Feudal Age Food, Castle is
  food at scale and *some* gold, Imperial is food and gold at scale. The age the scenario starts in
  or past stays free, and a fixed force still pays for no age. Feudal Age Food does not exist until
  #39: write the Feudal price so it accepts the item once it is pooled, or leave that branch for
  #39 and say so in the ticket.
- Build, Tech and Unit locations pay the age price of what they build, research or train. A Unit
  location also pays a training building, standing or paid, as paid fielding already does.
- Attila 3's Build Castle is the paid build of a Castle, plus the Castle price. Genghis 5's castles
  add the Castle price.
- Own Builder, and Own Repairer with no starting building, ask for a buildable building plus
  *some* of its cost.
- Delete the #37 allowlist entries these changes pay for.

**Acceptance criteria**

Prove each new test red at HEAD with `worlds/age2de/tools/red_check.py`, and tick each box with its
evidence.

- [ ] Reach Feudal is in logic with *some* food and no easy food. Reach Castle is in logic with

- [ ] A Castle-start scenario's Build, Tech and Unit locations ask no Feudal or Castle price.
- [ ] Attila 3's Build Castle asks the Castle's cost and the Castle price. Genghis 5's castles ask
      the Castle price.
- [ ] A Unit location asks the unit's age price and a training building, standing or paid.
- [ ] Own Builder and Own Repairer (with no starting building) ask *some* of a buildable building.
- [ ] The #37 allowlist has lost every entry for the sites above, and the test is green.
- [ ] The fill gate (`fuzz/bench/README.md`) passes, and its row is recorded under Stored runs.

**Out of scope**

- Anything inside the economy: routes, needs, seed items. That is #39 and #40.
- `test/easy_source_answers.json`. Do not recapture it ([#36](36-pre-commit-test-curation.md)).
