# 53: Units handed over reach logic without the access needed to reach them

**Reported:** 2026-10-29, playtesting

**Status:** needs-info

**Related:** [#51](51-in-game-checks.md) (the G1-1 row and its fix),
[#54](54-three-tribes-unreachable.md)

## Report

Two cases found in one session, both the same shape: a **scenario item** hands P1 units, logic
counts the units as obtained, and the thing the game actually requires before the hand-over
happens is not modelled.

1. **Own Monk** comes into logic with only Ungirrad's camp and no Kereyid (blue) camp. The
   Ungirrads cannot be recruited without the Kereyids first, so that Monk is not accessible.
2. **Own Scorpion** comes into logic from Recruits Across the River while P1 has no Transport
   to reach them.

The reporter's conclusion, and the right scope for this ticket: **every case where units are
handed over needs examining, and each needs its logical access.** Patching these two leaves the
same bug wherever else the pattern appears.

## Audit (structure_verified against the scenario files)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

### Case 2 is the clearest, because the trigger spells out the requirement

`AP Give Recruits` in `AP_Joan_1.aoe2scenario` fires on **two** conditions, not one:

| kind | detail |
| --- | --- |
| condition | `script_call HasJoan1Recruits();` - the item |
| condition | `objects_in_area` source_player 1, object 430, quantity 1, area `(30,100)-(39,110)` |
| effect | `change_ownership` P5 to P1 over `(33,104)-(36,106)` |

Object **430 is `HJODA`, Joan of Arc herself**. So the units change hands only once Joan is
standing on the far bank, which in Joan 1 means crossing the river.

`Joan1Rules` already knows this and gates the **location** correctly:

```python
can_cross_water = (Has(Age2ItemData.AP_JOAN_1_TRANSPORT.item_name)
                   & self.logic.units.survives_caveman(Age2UnitData.TRANSPORT_SHIP))
self.world.set_rule(self.locations[Age2ScenarioLocationData.JOAN1_RECRUITS], can_cross_water)
```

What is missing is the same gate on the **units the item yields**. `AP_JOAN_1_RECRUITS` (4007)
has roster Crossbowman x6, **Scorpion x2**, so Own Scorpion follows the item rather than the
crossing. The location is gated and the unit is not: that gap is the bug, and it is not specific
to Joan 1.

### Case 1 is the same shape with a different prerequisite

Two Genghis 1 tribes carry a Monk (`items/Items.py`):

| item | id | roster | `in_logic` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `AP_GENGHIS_1_KEREYID_TRIBE` | 4020 | Cavalry Archer x8, **Monk x2** | `True` |
| `AP_GENGHIS_1_UNGIRRAD_TRIBE` | 4013 | Mangudai x3, **Monk x1** | not set, so `False` |

The Ungirrad grant is trigger 88 `AP Give Grey Tribe`: `change_ownership` from P8 over
`(54,91)-(62,98)`, which holds 4 Mangudai and 1 Monk. The recruitment order established while
playing #51's G1-1 row is that the Ungirrads require the Kereyids first, so every route to that
Monk also runs through the Kereyids.

`Genghis1Rules` already expresses the intended shape everywhere else it gates:

```python
has_monk: Rule = (Has(Age2ItemData.AP_GENGHIS_1_KEREYID_TRIBE.item_name)
                  & self.logic.units.survives_caveman(Age2UnitData.MONK))
```

So here too the Kereyid requirement is the house style, and what needs finding is the path by
which Own Monk reaches logic without it.

### The shared cause

In both cases the scenario's hand-over trigger carries a condition beyond the AP item - Joan on
the far bank, the Kereyids recruited first - and that condition is invisible to
`is_granted` / `obtains_unit`, which follow the item's roster.

## What this needs

1. **Enumerate every unit hand-over.** Each AP item whose roster lists units, and for each the
   scenario trigger that actually performs the `change_ownership` or `remove_object`, with its
   full condition list. `kit scen describe --section triggers --full --json` gives the conditions
   directly; the Joan 1 and Genghis 1 reads above are the pattern.
2. **For each, name the access it really needs** - water access, a prior tribe, a defeated
   player, standing in an area - and whether logic models it today.
3. **Decide where that lives.** Both symptoms come from the same missing idea, so one answer is
   likely better than a patch per item. Options worth weighing: carry the access rule on the item
   so `is_granted` picks it up, or keep it in the scenario's logic class as
   `Genghis1Rules.has_monk` already does for its own cases.
4. Check what `in_logic` gates (`items/Items.py:163`). Four of the five Genghis 1 tribes are
   `False` and only the Kereyids are `True`, which may be deliberate or may be part of this.

Severity: this direction is the dangerous one. A unit in logic that the game cannot hand over can
strand a seed, the same way #54 can.

## Triage Notes

**What we've established so far:**

- Mechanism: `default_mercenary_grants` ORs `Has(item)` onto every roster unit with no access
  gate. It is deleted. Every hand-over rule is authored by hand per scenario in
  `StartingState.obtains_unit`, case by case, with no generic per-item gate. A test pins the
  unit list against the authored rules for every scenario.
- Authored rules stay caveman-free: `ScenarioUnitLogic.is_granted` ANDs `survives_caveman`
  (pinned by `test_scenario_rules.py:410-412`).
- Gates shared by a location and a grant live once, on the `StartingState`, and the
  `*Rules` class reads them from there.
- Rulings:
  - **J1**: `can_cross_water` moves to the state. `SCORPION = Has(RECRUITS) & can_cross_water`,
    `CROSSBOWMAN = Has(CROSSBOWMEN) | (Has(RECRUITS) & can_cross_water)`. Ram Army is bare:
    `PIKEMAN`/`CAPPED_RAM = Has(RAM)`, `MAN_AT_ARMS = Has(SWORDSMEN) | Has(RAM)`.
  - **G1**: `has_monk` and `can_slay_ornlu` move to the state. `CAVALRY_ARCHER`/`LIGHT_CAVALRY`
    stay `True_()`, `MONK = Has(KEREYID)`, `MANGUDAI = Has(UNGIRRAD) & has_monk`,
    `CAMEL_RIDER = Has(UIGHUR) & can_slay_ornlu`.
  - **G3**: Engineers and Bombards both need `restorable_transport` (the Transport blocks the
    villagers; the Bombards need it too). `engineers = Has(ENGINEERS) & restorable_transport`
    for Villagers, Battering Ram, Rocket Cart. `bombards_reached = Has(BOMBARDS) &
    restorable_transport` for `BOMBARD_CANNON`; `restorable_bombards` builds on
    `bombards_reached`; `starts_with_building[SIEGE_WORKSHOP] = bombards_reached | engineers`.
  - **A2 Scythian Troop**: `Has(SCYTHIAN_TROOP) & purple_vils` (the trade needs 6 villagers),
    for Mangudai, Capped Ram, Onager. The Eastern Roman defeat rule picks it up.
  - Bare `Has(item)`: G5 Hermits (Monk, Elite Leitis), A1 Roman Villagers (unchanged), A1
    Mangudai, A2 Dyrrhachium Prisoners (Tarkan, Hussar), J5 Loyalists, J5 King's Troop, J6
    Artillery, G1 Kereyid, G6 Subotai's Army (Subotai, Khan, Saboteur; the 2 Hunting Wolves
    are ignored).
- J6 Gaia starts Post-Imperial (`starting_age = 6`), as does G5. That is the likely reason
  the J6 Lone Swordsman (placed as Militia, unit 74) shows as a Champion. J6 never obtains
  Militia except through caveman, so the current `obtains_unit[MILITIA] = army` is wrong, and
  J6 Army as authored is wrong. Neither "drop Gaia to Castle" nor "author the top tiers" is
  the fix.
