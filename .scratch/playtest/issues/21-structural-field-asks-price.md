### Grilling (2026-10-22)

- **Verified from code.** `_field` asks `economy.can_sustain(...)` in both forms. The structural
  form's one caller, `_protecting_boats`, sits behind `can_protect_boats`, which the `"afloat"` tier
  gate (deep fish, whales) and the `"shoreline"` gate (shore fish, oysters) ask. The navy's lowest
  tier costs wood and gold, so cogs (easy wood by partner trade) already pay for the guard today.
- **Latent loop.** No shipped scenario (Attila, Joan, Genghis) has oysters or whales. Barbarossa
  (`oyster_count=1400`) and Saladin (`1275`) do: easy gold -> shoreline gate -> guard ->
  `can_sustain({gold})` -> easy gold, which `ScenarioQuestion.answered` refuses with
  `RecursionError` at rule build.
- **Conflict with #19.** #19's leaf part "asks no easy source" and lists tier gates as leaf, but the
  afloat and shoreline gates ask easy sources through the guard.
- **Ruling: the guard is an economy seed need.** Guarding boats is part of what the fishing, oyster
  and whale routes need before they yield, not a price and not free. The guard's navy cost becomes
  **route needs** of those routes, resolved by #19's fixpoint, so any route (cogs included) may pay
  for it and the oyster/whale loop resolves instead of raising. The structural form of `_field` then
  pays for nothing (ADR-0002); the navy's resources move out of it into the route needs.
- **Ruling: the guard needs *some*, not *at scale*.** Enough wood and gold to put one navy out once
  (a bank, relic gold, any route of *some*). That changes answers (guarding gets easier, fishing may
  enter logic earlier), so it is a catalog change for #22 with #22's fill measurement. #19 still
  has to place the guard somewhere to keep its leaf part clean: there it reproduces today's answers
  as route needs *at scale*, and #22 lowers them to *some*.
- **Ruling: no bank pays for the guard.** The guard's *some* wood and gold must come from a route
  yielding them (a few villagers chopping, relics, a little mining, cogs, a scenario's *some* hook),
  never from banked resources (`HasResourceAmount`). So the guard has no authored bank amount,
  unlike the Fishing Ship (75 wood) or cog (400 wood, 100 gold) seeds.
