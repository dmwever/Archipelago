# 24: Items that seed the economy's buildings and units

**Reported:** 2026-10-23, raised by the maintainer while grilling [#22](22-economy-route-catalog.md)

**Status:** needs-triage

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during grilling.*

The base is paid by its own seed items (`TOWN_CENTER_WOOD`, `TOWN_CENTER_STONE`,
`STARTING_VILLAGER_FOOD`), but every other building and unit an economy seed asks for is free: #17
keeps the economy structural (`has_building_structurally`), so a camp, Dock, Market or Monastery an
economy route leans on is never paid for, and neither is the Monk that collects relics.

The maintainer's idea: items, in the shape of the Town Center items, that seed those buildings and
units, so an economy route's seed can pay for them without asking an easy source (which would close
the cycle #17 avoided). Candidates named so far: the camps (Lumber Camp, Mining Camp, Mill), the
Dock, the Market, the Monastery and the Monk.

## Open questions

- Which buildings and units get a seed item, and is it one item per building or one per resource?
- What does each item hold: the building's full cost, or a bank that a route of *some* tops up?
- Do they replace the structural form inside economy seeds, or sit beside it as another way in?
- Pool impact: how many items, which classification, and the fill gate.
