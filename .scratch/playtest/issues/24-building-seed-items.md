# 24: Items that seed the economy's buildings and units

**Reported:** 2026-10-23, raised by the maintainer while grilling [#22](22-economy-route-catalog.md)

**Status:** done

**Related:** [#17](17-buildable-buildings-unpaid.md), [#19](19-trade-cogs-without-base.md), [#22](22-economy-route-catalog.md), [#23](23-land-defence-unpaid.md)

## Report

> *This was generated by AI during grilling.*

The base is paid by its own seed items (`TOWN_CENTER_WOOD`, `TOWN_CENTER_STONE`,
`STARTING_VILLAGER_FOOD`), but every other building and unit an economy seed asks for is free: #17
keeps the economy structural (`has_building_structurally`), so a camp, Dock, Market or Monastery an
economy route leans on is never paid for, and neither is the Monk that collects relics.

The maintainer's idea: items, in the shape of the Town Center items, that seed those buildings and
units, so an economy route's seed can pay for them without asking an easy source (which would close
the cycle #17 avoids). Candidates named so far: the camps (Lumber Camp, Mining Camp, Mill), the
Dock, the Market, the Monastery and the Monk.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-26 after grilling (see Comments).

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Fund the buildings an economy route is built on - by a seed item where the building
opens an economy from nothing, by *some* wood where it does not - so a route of *some* stops
starting on a free building.

**Current behavior**

A villager gathering *some* of anything needs a dropsite, and every dropsite is structural and
therefore free. With no Town Center standing, a free Lumber Camp is all that stands between a
scenario and *some* wood; a free Mining Camp is all that stands between it and *some* gold. Those
two are the Trade Cog economy seed, and a cog route is the only route an economy seed of *some* can
start (ADR-0003), so the whole no-base economy currently opens on buildings nobody paid for. The
same holds for a free Mill or Folwark and *some* food, a free Dock and the cog route, a free Market
and market trade, and a free Monastery and relic gold.

Three things already fund themselves and are not in question: the base, through the Town Center
items; a defence, through `can_pay_some` of its own cost (#23); and a location, through its price
(ADR-0002).

**Desired behavior**

*Capital is not an operating input.* A route's `EconomyRoute.needs` state what must already be
yielding before the producer runs. They do not buy the building the producer stands in, which does
not exist yet. Capital comes from outside the economy, which is what keeps #17's cycle closed.

*Two funding strengths, chosen by whether the building opens an economy from nothing.*

- **A seed item** funds a building that can start an economy with nothing else running: the Lumber
  Camp and Mule Cart (*some* wood with no Town Center), the Dock (the cog route) and the Market
  (market trade and the trade-cart route). These are capital, held as items, exactly as the Town
  Center is.
- **`needs={WOOD: SOME}`** funds a building that only opens a resource inside an economy that is
  already running: the Mining Camp (gold and stone), the Mill and Folwark (food), and the Monastery
  (relics). Some other route must already be yielding wood; `TestARouteNeverSeedsItself` guarantees
  it is a different route.
- **Nothing** funds a building whose route already demands more than the building costs through a
  standing need - the Fish Trap and the boat dropsite both sit behind `WOOD: AT_SCALE`.

*A standing building is free.* Funding takes the shape the base already uses: a building that
starts standing costs nothing, and only a built one asks for its seed. Structural stays structural -
no route reaches `economy.can_pay`.

*The seed is asked wherever the building is built.* A seeded building asks for its seed on every
path that puts it up, not only the path that motivated it. The Dock funded for the cog route is
therefore also funded when a Fisherman drops off at it, and the Lumber Camp funded for *some* wood
is also funded as the at-scale wood camp.

*The Town Center branch of a dropsite carries no funding.* A dropsite is a disjunction and the Town
Center branch needs no purchase, so a route charged for a camp must not charge a player whose Town
Center is doing the work. Each villager route of *some* therefore becomes several explicit routes -
a Town Center variant carrying nothing, and one variant per camp carrying that camp's funding.

*The route list is hand-written.* Routes are declared one by one, not generated. The combinatorial
expansion that currently produces the banked trade routes is replaced by its six routes written out,
so a route can be changed by editing it.

*The Mule Cart's food is a bank.* The Mule Cart costs wood and food; its seed item carries the wood
only, and its food is a bank read off the building's own cost. A bank rather than a need matters
here: the Mule Cart is a hunt dropsite, so a food *need* would make its hunt variant need what that
variant yields and go permanently false.

*A seed item is pooled only where it can fund something.* With partner trade off there is no cog or
cart route; with market trade off there is no market route. An item whose routes cannot run this
seed is not pooled, and does not count toward the starting-wood invariant.

*Starting wood still sums to 1000.* The rolled starting-wood target is whatever is left of 1000
after the seed items this seed actually pools, rather than a constant minus one item.

**Key interfaces**

- `TCResources` is renamed `SeedResources`, keeping its `progression` classification. It gains a
  field naming what it seeds, typed as the building data class, so the three Town Center items and
  the four new ones are one kind of thing.
- A new module under the world's `connections` package wires each funded building to its seed item,
  the way game costs are wired onto the data classes today, with import-time asserts that every
  seed item funds something real and nothing is funded twice. The building data class gains a
  nullable seed-item field beside its cost.
- `ScenarioLogic` gains one question for "this building, standing or seeded", which is what the
  routes ask instead of `has_building_structurally` for a funded building.
- `EconomyRoute` is unchanged. What changes is that the routes are enumerated explicitly and the
  banked-route expansion helper is deleted.
- `HasResourceAmount` gains an exclusion set of item names, filtered out after its cached
  contributor scan and also dropped from `item_dependencies`, so local start does not offer an
  excluded item as a way to reach the amount. Exactly one caller uses it: the cog route's wood
  bank excludes the Dock's seed item, because that item is already buying the Dock the same rule
  asks for. No other bank and seeded building share a resource inside one rule.
- The resource pool's guaranteed floor stops being "every `SeedResources` item" and becomes "every
  `SeedResources` item whose routes can run under these options". The starting-wood target is
  computed from that floor.
- Four new progression items at ids 1032-1035, contiguous, each granting its resource in game
  through a case in the item handler's progression switch and a one-line resource-grant function,
  in the shape the Town Center items already use. That switch has no default, so a missing case is
  silent.

**Funded buildings and amounts**

| Building | Seed item | Pooled when |
|---|---|---|
| Lumber Camp | 100 wood | always |
| Mule Cart | 80 wood | always |
| Dock | 150 wood | `partner_trade` is not `none` |
| Market | 175 wood | `market_trade` is on, or `partner_trade` is not `none` |

Resulting guaranteed starting wood, and the rolled target that holds the 1000 invariant:

| `market_trade` | `partner_trade` | Seed wood | Rolled wood target |
|---|---|---|---|
| on | allies / allies+enemies | 780 | 220 |
| off | allies / allies+enemies | 780 | 220 |
| on | none | 630 | 370 |
| off | none | 455 | 545 |

The food target is unchanged: the Mule Cart's food is a bank, not an item.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Grilling session, 2026-10-26.

Rulings worth keeping, because each reversed a plausible-looking alternative:

- **A route's `needs` do not pay for the buildings in its own economy seed.** The market cannot pay
  for its own wood - it does not exist yet. This killed the reading that the Market, the boat Dock
  and the Fish Trap were already funded by their routes' standing `WOOD: AT_SCALE` need, and with
  it the conclusion that this ticket had nothing to do.
- **Relics is the exception, and it is not the same thing.** `needs={WOOD: SOME}` on the relic route
  is not the route funding itself: relics yields gold and needs wood, so there is no self-reference,
  and the fixpoint sources the wood from a different route. `can_pay_some` is unavailable there -
  relics lives in the ungated layer that `has_some_source` resolves, so asking it would be the
  question asking itself.
- **The Mining Camp and Monastery dropped off the item list for the same reason.** Both open a
  resource inside an economy that is already running; only a building that can start one from
  nothing needs capital.
- **The bank exclusion is rule-local, not a flag on the item.** The Dock seed should count toward
  local start and everything else, and be invisible only to the bank inside the rule it is already
  buying a Dock for. An enumeration of the world's three bank sites confirmed the cog route is the
  only place a seeded building and a bank meet over one resource.
- **Route variants over a loop.** The Town Center branch of a dropsite needs its own route so the
  camp's funding does not fall on a player who needs no camp, and those variants - like the trade
  routes - are written out rather than generated, so one can be changed by editing it.
- **The Mule Cart's food is a bank, not a need.** A need would make its hunt variant need what it
  yields and go permanently false.

> *This was generated by AI during implementation.* 2026-10-26.

Implemented in both repos. The fill gate ran once the bench and the fixed seeds were in the repo
(see the criterion above).

Ruled by the maintainer after implementation:

- **With `partner_trade: none` the Dock is any other dropsite.** "The seed is asked wherever the
  building is built" met "Dock pooled when `partner_trade` is not `none`", leaving a built Dock
  unfundable for fishermen and oyster gatherers. Ruling: no pooling - a seed item stays pooled
  only for the routes that would otherwise fund themselves - and the Dock falls back to the Mill's
  funding. "Fishermen at a Dock" and "Oyster Gatherers at a Dock" need *some* wood, the at-scale
  fisherman camp asks for the Dock structurally, and fishing boats drop there freely as before.
  Red: `test_seed_items.py::TestWithNoPartnerTradeTheDockIsFundedLikeAMill` (three red, three
  held before, including that with partner trade on fishermen still ask for the Dock seed). The
  pinned easy-source answers did not move.
- **`STARTING_VILLAGER_FOOD` (1024) now grants its food in game.** It had no case in
  `GiveProgressionItem`, whose switch has no default, so it granted nothing. 1024 once held
  Genghis 1's Blue Force before that moved to 1025; villager food never had a case.
  `StartingVillagerFood()` adds the 150 food.

Also noted, not changed:

- The house style memory says "seed" means only the generation seed, and `CONTEXT.md` lists
  *seed resources* under Avoid; the ticket's `SeedResources` and **Seed item** naming was kept as
  briefed.
- No test couples the XS grant amounts (100, 80 + 20 food, 150, 175) to `Items.py`.
