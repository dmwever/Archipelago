# 16: Under caveman, are scenario-granted ships and carts knocked down?

**Reported:** 2026-10-22, split from [issue 01](01-caveman-scenario-rules.md) during triage

**Status:** needs-info

**Blocked by:** #10, for the in-game test only. See *Triage Notes (2026-10-27)*.

## Report

Issue 01's audit listed scenario rules that count on units a trigger hands over: Transport Ships
(Joan 1, Joan 3, Genghis 3, the Joan 2 Dock route), Trade Carts with their escort (Joan 2), and the
French Army (Joan 6). The first ruling was that ships and carts are not cavemanified in game, which
would make those rows harmless. In the playtest, transports arrived in Joan 1 and Trade Carts in
Joan 2 without being knocked down, but both lines may already have been unlocked at the time.

### Triage Notes (2026-10-22)

**What we've established so far:**

- `ApplyCaveman` (`Caveman.xs:207`) leaves a unit alone only when:
  - its type is not in the unit table;
  - its row is `cavemanExempt` (villager lines only, `UnitData.is_caveman_exempt`);
  - it is on the immune list (mercenaries, `MercenarySpawn.xs:77`);
  - its type is exempted with `ExemptUnitTypeFromCaveman`, which only the Flemish Militia trap uses.
    `CavemanExemption()` is empty in the scenarios checked.
- The Transport Ship Line (834) and Trade Cart Line (819) are unit-line locations (`UnitLines.py`).
  With unitsanity on, their rows are in the table, and a row stays `locked` until its items arrive
  (`setUnitHidden`, `Unitsanity.xs:157`).
- A locked row has no unlocked tier, so `cavemanTarget` falls back to `CAVEMAN_MILITIA`. Going by
  the code, a trigger-granted Transport Ship whose line is still locked becomes a Militia on the
  water, or is deleted if that spawn fails. A Trade Cart becomes a Militia. This comes from reading
  code only; it has not been reproduced.
- With unitsanity off, these rows are not in the table, so caveman leaves the units alone.
- The playtest observations so far cannot tell "exempt" apart from "already unlocked".

**What we still need from you (@reporter):**

- A caveman seed with **unitsanity on**, where the **Transport Ship Line** item has **not** been
  received: play Joan 1 up to the transport hand-over. Do the transports stay ships, become
  Militia, or vanish?
- The same for Joan 2 with the **Trade Cart Line** item not received, at the Trade Cart hand-over.
- The yaml (or at least `caveman`, `unitsanity`, `unitsanity_items`) for that seed.

**What happens next, depending on the answer:**

- **Knocked down, and that is acceptable:** logic bug. The issue 01 rows for Transport Ship, Trade
  Cart and French Army need the same `NOT_CAVEMAN | can_restore(unit)` gate as the monk fix.
- **Knocked down, and it should not be:** game-side bug. Widen the caveman exemption to ships and
  carts (`UnitData.is_caveman_exempt`, which feeds the XS `cavemanExempt` flag). Logic stays as it is.
- **Not knocked down:** find the mechanism that exempts them and record it in `CONTEXT.md`.

### Triage Notes (2026-10-27)

**Run the in-game test after [#10](10-caveman-sweep-delayed.md) lands.**

- #10 found that the unitsanity rule skips cavemanify on any second where P1's unit count changed.
  It also never notices a unit gained in the same second another is lost.
- A trigger hand-over is exactly such a moment: several units arrive at once, often in the middle
  of a scenario's opening.
- So "the transports stayed ships" has a third explanation besides exempt and already unlocked:
  the sweep simply hadn't run yet. The original playtest observations can't rule that out either.
- Once #10 lands, every new P1 unit is cavemanified within 1 game second. A ship or cart that is
  still itself a few seconds after the hand-over then really was left alone.
- The questions above stand unchanged. Only their timing moves.
