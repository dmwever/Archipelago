After #23, resources out on the map (`Tier.OPEN`, the shoreline) are worked only behind a built
defence, paid by routes of *some*. The maintainer's idea: a **unit** can defend the resources it
needs at *some*, boosting that route to *at scale*.

### Triage Notes (2026-10-26)

- **Premise moved with #23.** A route of *some* no longer asks the open-ground gate
  (`open_resource_gates()`); only routes at scale do (`tier_gates()`: a built defence, with the
  base asked by the route). So the question is whether an army is a way through the at-scale gate.
- **Not implemented.** `can_defend_resources()` -> `_fortified()` asks buildings only. The water has
  the pattern already: `can_guard_boats()` fields each navy line structurally and pays
  `can_pay_some` of the line's own cost.
- No `.out-of-scope/` exists; no prior rejection.

### Grilling (2026-10-26)

- **A way through the open-ground gate, beside a built defence.** The base is still asked; an army
  does not stand in for it.
- **Any land military line** (`UnitRole.military`) P1 can train, Or'd, as `can_guard_boats()` does
  the navy. No threat-specific counter.
- **Costs *some* of the line's own cost**, no bank, as every defence.
- **Holds all the open ground**, every resource there, not only what the line costs. The gates take
  no parameter.
- **Open ground only.** The shoreline keeps built defences and the navy; afloat stays navy-only.
- **Defence widened** in `CONTEXT.md` to an army on open ground; ADR-0002 gains a consequence.
- **A starting or granted army holds nothing**: only a line P1 can train counts.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Approved 2026-10-26 after grilling.

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** An army P1 can train holds open ground at scale, beside a built defence

**Current behavior:**
Routes of *some* ask `ScenarioResourceLogic.open_resource_gates()`, whose open tier is free. Routes
at scale ask `tier_gates()`, whose land gate for `Tier.OPEN` is `can_defend_resources()` ->
`ScenarioBuildingLogic._fortified()`: built defences only (more Town Centers, a strongpoint, or a
wall with a tower), each `can_build_structurally(B) & can_pay_some(B.cost)`. No unit can hold open
ground. On the water, `ScenarioMilitaryLogic.can_guard_boats()` already holds the afloat and
shoreline gates with an army: each navy line `can_field_structurally(line)` and
`can_pay_some(resources_to_sustain(line))`, Or'd, with water access.

**Desired behavior:** see **Defence** in `CONTEXT.md` and ADR-0002's "Holding open ground with an
army" consequence (both already updated).
- **The land gate for `Tier.OPEN` holds through a built defence or an army.** The army is the land
  sibling of `can_guard_boats()`: for each `UnitRole.military` line,
  `can_field_structurally(line) & can_pay_some(resources_to_sustain(line))`, Or'd. A line no tier
  of which this scenario can field drops out, as in `can_guard_boats()`.
- **Every resource on open ground**: the army opens the gate, not a resource. The gates keep
  taking no parameter.
- **Open ground only.** The shoreline gate (`can_hold_a_shoreline()` or the navy) and its
  "Mill with `is_fortified()`" way do not take the army; the afloat gate stays navy-only. So the
  army joins the land gate's own question, not `is_fortified()`/`_fortified()` as the shoreline
  reads them.
- **Only an army P1 can train.** `can_field_structurally` asks training (items, upgrade tech, a
  training building, the age), never `obtains_unit`; keep it that way. A starting or granted army
  holds nothing.
- **Paid at *some*, never a bank**, through `can_pay_some`, which resolves against the ungated
  routes. A gate must not ask the full economy (`RecursionError`, ADR-0003).
- **The base is still asked** by the at-scale villager routes, unchanged.
- **No route change.** No new routes, no `EconomyRoute.needs`, no generated names; the catalog
  stays the size `tools/route_catalog.py` pins.
- `/explain` shows the army as its own named way under "can defend resources" (for example "can
  hold open ground with an army"), beside the built defences.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_defend_resources` / `_defend_open_ground`: the land gate; gains the
  army way.
- `ScenarioMilitaryLogic`: a land sibling of `can_guard_boats()` returning a `Rule`.
- `ScenarioBuildingLogic.is_fortified`, `_fortified`, `can_hold_a_shoreline`: unchanged in
  meaning; the shoreline must not pick up the army.
- `ScenarioUnitLogic.can_field_structurally`, `resources_to_sustain`; `ScenarioResourceLogic.can_pay_some`.
