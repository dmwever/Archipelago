## Report

28 lines of `test/never_bare_allowlist.txt` at #40's close read
`<scenario> runs Market Trade: <scenario> can build a Market (unpaid: food, stone)` (or `food` alone).
The single "Market Trade" route waits on a Market through `can_trade_at_market` →
`has_seeded_building(MARKET)`. The Market is a Feudal building, so the route waits on Feudal, and
nothing pays the Feudal price (food) or the advance's Town Center (stone). #39 paid Feudal for
Trade Carts, Trade Cogs, Fish Traps and relics; Market Trade was missed.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.* Ruling: the maintainer, 2026-10-28 (#32's grilling, Q5).

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Split Market Trade into the three Feudal versions the Trade Cart routes have, and pool
Feudal Age Food for it.

**Current behavior**

One "Market Trade" route, leaf `can_trade_at_market()`, needs `wood: at scale`. In a scenario starting
below Feudal it asks the Market's Feudal age with no price and no advance.

**Desired behavior**

Market Trade comes in the versions the Trade Cart routes are written in, by name and shape:

- **started in Feudal**: the leaf adds `ages.has_started_in(FEUDAL)`; needs unchanged.
- **advanced to Feudal**: the leaf adds the paid Town Center (`has_building(TOWN_CENTER)`); needs add
  `food: some`.
- **on Feudal Age Food**: the leaf adds the Feudal seed item, the paid Town Center, and the age-up
  buildings standing or seeded (`ages.has_standing_or_seeded_age_up_buildings(FEUDAL)`); needs
  unchanged.

A version that cannot apply to a scenario resolves away as the trade versions do. With `market_trade`
off, none exists.

**Feudal Age Food is pooled for Market Trade too.** The #24 rule pools it only where an enabled
scenario starting below Feudal has a route that waits on Feudal (a reachable trade partner under
`partner_trade`, or water for Fish Traps). With `market_trade` on, any enabled scenario starting below
Feudal whose civilisation builds a Market now counts.

**Key interfaces**

- `ScenarioRoutesLogic.market_routes`: one route becomes three.
- The Feudal Age Food pooling rule (#24's): one more condition.

**Acceptance criteria**

Prove each new test red at HEAD with `worlds/age2de/tools/red_check.py`, and tick each box with its
evidence.

- [ ] A test: in a scenario starting in Feudal or later, Market Trade asks no food need.
- [ ] A test: Feudal Age Food is pooled in a world whose only Feudal-waiting route is Market Trade,
