# 56: Joan 2 does not remove P1's Trade Carts after they reach Orleans

**Reported:** 2026-10-30, playtesting

**Status:** needs-info

## Report

Joan of Arc 2 does not remove P1's Trade Carts after bringing them to Orleans. The player keeps
all six.

## Audit (structure_verified against `AP_Joan_2.aoe2scenario`)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

### The triggers named "Removed" do not remove anything

This is the thing most likely to mislead whoever picks this up. `Trade Cart 1 Removed` through
`Trade Cart 6 Removed` (t48-t53) are **not** a consumption step. They are the other half of a
counter toggle:

| trigger | enabled | condition | effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| t41 `Trade Cart 1 Arrives` | yes | cart 22349 brought **into** `(117,63)-(125,70)` | `TradeCarts += 1`, activate t48 |
| t48 `Trade Cart 1 Removed` | no | cart 22349 brought into that area, **inverted** | `TradeCarts -= 1`, activate t41 |

So "removed" means removed from the tally, when a cart *leaves* Orleans. Each pair re-arms the
other, and the variable tracks how many carts are standing in Orleans right now. Nothing in this
machinery deletes a cart, and that is vanilla behaviour, not something the AP conversion changed.

The AP location reads the same variable:

| trigger | condition | effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| t58 `AP Bring Trade Carts to Orleans` | `TradeCarts == 6` | `script_call BringCartsToOrleans();` |

And the carts themselves are an AP grant:

| trigger | condition | effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| t67 `AP Give Trade Carts` | `HasJoan2Carts();` + Joan in `(14,88)-(29,100)` | `change_ownership` Gaia to P1 over `(9,89)-(23,96)` |

So AP hands the carts over from Gaia, the player escorts them, the location fires when all six
are in Orleans at once, and the six carts remain P1's afterwards.

### What logic currently believes

`logic/joan/joan_2.py:44` says the player obtains them:

```python
self.obtains_unit[U.TRADE_CART] = has_carts
```

which puts **Own Trade Cart** in logic for Joan 2 whenever `AP_JOAN_2_TRADE_CARTS` is held. That
matches the game as it behaves today, so logic and game currently agree.

The economy is not affected either way: `can_run_trade_carts` asks
`has_unit_items(Age2UnitData.TRADE_CART)` - the unit **item**, not these granted carts - so a
trade route is not opened by keeping them.

## Needs info

The code is self-consistent, so what is wrong depends on the intent, which is not written down
anywhere I can find:

1. **If the carts should be consumed on delivery** (they are supplies for a besieged city), then
   the fix is a new AP trigger removing them when `BringCartsToOrleans` fires, **and**
   `obtains_unit[U.TRADE_CART]` in `joan_2.py` becomes wrong - Own Trade Cart would no longer be
   satisfiable there, because the player never durably owns one.
2. **If the carts should stay** and the report is really about something downstream - a trade
   route the player should not have, or an Own Trade Cart check that feels unearned - then the
   fix is elsewhere and this ticket should say which.

Reading 1 changes a logic rule as well as the scenario, so it is worth settling before anyone
touches either.

One note either way: with six Trade Carts, a Market and a trade partner, the player can run
trade in game without holding the Trade Cart item, while logic gates the route on that item.
That is game ahead of logic, which cannot strand a seed, but it is a divergence and it is the
kind of thing reading 2 might be pointing at.
