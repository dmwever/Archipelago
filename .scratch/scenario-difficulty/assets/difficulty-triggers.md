# Difficulty-conditional triggers in the 18 AP scenarios

Source: `Ageipelago/age 2 files/resources/_common/scenario/AP_*.aoe2scenario` on `scenario-difficulty`.
Raw dump: [`difficulty-triggers.json`](difficulty-triggers.json), regenerated by
[`tools/audit_triggers.py`](tools/audit_triggers.py) (run with `python -I`, any cwd). That script covers mechanism 1 only; the AI-signal rows below were read by hand from the scenarios and `ai-scripts/` (gitignored, proprietary; if missing, extract it with `python -I tools/dump_ai.py`). Ids are resolved
from the vendored `worlds/age2de/AoE2ScenarioParser/datasets/`. Object coordinates and owners are
read from `unit_manager`.

## Two mechanisms

1. **Difficulty Level condition** (`ConditionId.DIFFICULTY_LEVEL` = 19, attribute `quantity` = a
   `DifficultyLevel` value: easiest 4, standard 3, moderate 2, hard 1, hardest 0, extreme -1).
   30 triggers in 13 scenarios.
2. **AI Signal condition** (`ConditionId.AI_SIGNAL`), where the scenario's AI script runs
   `(set-signal N)` from a `(difficulty …)` rule. 8 triggers in 5 scenarios. The trigger itself has
   no difficulty condition, so a scan for condition 19 misses them. Genghis 1's "Hard Difficulty"
   is one of these.

### What `Difficulty Level q=N` means (inferred)

The scenarios only make sense if the condition is true when **the game is at level N or easier**
(numeric level ≥ N). Evidence: Attila 4 "TM - Stop Cavalry Moderate" = `q=2 AND NOT q=3`, and
"…Hard" = `q=1 AND NOT q=3 AND NOT q=2`, which isolate exactly moderate and exactly hard under
this reading and can never be true under "N or harder". Joan 6 "Easy and Moderate" (`q=2`),
"Not Easy" (`NOT q=3`) and "Not Easy or Moderate" (`NOT q=2`) agree. The parser, the AoE2
knowledge base and the trigger catalogue all describe the condition without stating its
direction, so this is inferred from the scenario authors' trigger names.

Under that reading `q=3` ("Easy"/"Standard") also fires on easiest, and a lone `q=1` fires on
everything except hardest/extreme.

## Map content P1 interacts with (feeds *Does the tier touch logic or locations?*)

- **Genghis 1, hard:** the GAIA relic at (148,97) is removed. AP trigger 72 "AP Wolf Relic" checks
  a relic-carrying monk (286) in (146,96)–(148,98), the location *Pick Up the Relic Surrounded by
  Wolves* (`GEN1_RELIC_WOLF`, `OBJECTIVE_BRANCHING_ALL`). On hard that location cannot be checked.
  It is triggered by the AI's signal, so it changes with the AI tier, not the trigger audit alone.
- **Free P1 techs on easy/easiest:** Loom (Attila 1), Murder Holes (Attila 1–5). Under techsanity
  these bypass the AP item for that scenario.
- **Free P1 buildings on easy/easiest:** Attila 6 Castle + two Mining Camps, relevant to
  `shuffle_buildings` (P1 owns a Castle without the item).
- **Free P1 units + techs:** Genghis 6 Siege Onagers + Onager/Siege Onager research.
- **Diplomacy:** Joan 5 easy turns P3 Neutral towards P1.
- **Capturable GAIA siege:** Joan 6 removes GAIA Bombard Cannons / Trebuchet on moderate+ and hard+.
- **Terrain:** Joan 3 easy/moderate adds oak trees at (167,141–142) (likely a path block).
- **Enemy strength only** (no P1 content): everything else.

## Anomalies for the decision tickets

- **Condition direction is inferred**, not sourced. Ticket *Verify Difficulty Level condition direction in game* tests it, along with the inverted AI Signal and owner-mismatch cases below.
- **Genghis 6 "Additional Siege Onagers"** fires only on extreme under the inferred reading,
  which contradicts its evident purpose (help P1 on easy). Either the inferred reading is wrong
  for inverted conditions, or this trigger is effectively dead in the campaign today.
- **`== normal` gates two "easy mode" signals** (Genghis 4, Genghis 5). If `normal` is not a valid
  level symbol (ticket *Shared AI libraries and difficulty symbols*), those triggers never fire.
- **Inverted AI Signal** (Genghis 4 "Not Easy Mode"): true from game start until P2 sends 202,
  so it likely fires on every difficulty in the first trigger pass, before the AI runs.
- **Source-player / owner mismatch:** Attila 1 (Attila 10841, Watch Tower 22087), Attila 2
  (Mangonels 11666/11667) and Genghis 2 (all five units) select objects owned by a player other
  than the effect's source player. Whether selected-object effects filter by source player is not
  documented in the knowledge base; if they do, these effects are no-ops in the AP scenarios.
- **Joan 3 "Hard" (32)** is an empty trigger.
